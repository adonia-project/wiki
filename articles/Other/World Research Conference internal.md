# World Research Conference — Internal Notes

## Key Facts
- Recurring international scientific conference (Adonian equivalent of the Solvay Conferences)
- Brings together physicists, chemists, and other scientists from across Adonia
- Rotating host institutions in different countries
- 1933 edition: Ampuria, Volisania — site of the nuclear fission announcement
