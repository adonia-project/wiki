# Discovery of nuclear fission — Internal Notes

## Key Facts
- **Date**: 1932
- **Location**: University of Anping, Anping, Louyang (Zong commonwealth)
- **Discoverers**: 
  - **Tân Bûn-kong** (陳文光) — chemist, professor of chemistry at University of Anping. Born 2 April 1880 in Anping. Served in Second War of the Isthmus 1898–1899 (tail end, age 18). Zong nationalist, believed in Fosian Lurandian totality (ideology idealising total Zong/Fosian control of the Lurandian continent). Moved into chemistry after the war. Performed radiochemical separation and identified barium + krypton as fission products.
  - **Lîm Chiat-bêng** (林傑明) — physicist, research associate in physics department. Born 11 May 1894. Pacifist. Designed neutron bombardment apparatus, provided physical interpretation of fission, calculated energy release, identified chain reaction potential. 12 years younger than Tân.
- **Publication**: Presented at the World Research Conference in 1933, held in Ampuria, Volisania
- **Context**: Nuclear decay (radioactivity) research was being conducted across Adonia in this era — Volisania, Daras, Breisland, Balisca all had active research programmes

## Name Etymology
- **Tân Bûn-kong** (陳文光): Tân = 陳 (common Hokkien surname), Bûn = 文 (cultured/literary), Kong = 光 (light). "Cultured Light."
- **Lîm Chiat-bêng** (林傑明): Lîm = 林 (common Hokkien surname), Chiat = 傑 (outstanding), Bêng = 明 (bright). "Outstanding Brightness."

## Researcher Dynamic
- Tân Bûn-kong: Zong nationalist, Fosian Lurandian totality ideology, military veteran (Second War of the Isthmus 1898–1899), chemist. Saw nuclear fission's strategic potential for Zong dominance but also had ethical concerns about weapons.
- Lîm Chiat-bêng: Pacifist, physicist, opposed military applications. Focused on peaceful nuclear energy (reactor design). Growing rift with Tân over the military implications.
- The nationalist/pacifist tension is a central theme of the article and their relationship.

## Fosian Lurandian Totality
- Political ideology that idealises complete Fosian (specifically Zong) control over the Lurandian continent (the New World)
- Tân Bûn-kong subscribed to this ideology
- May need its own article in the future

## University of Anping
- Founded: 1605
- Located in Anping, Louyang (Zong commonwealth)
- One of the premier scientific institutions in the Zong commonwealth
- 1605 is during the Zong colonial period — Anping was Zong's second colony (founded 1474), and by 1605 it was a well-established colonial settlement
- Emperor Tianhao's court-in-exile was at Anping during the Yuanlong Restoration (c. 1585–1589), so the university was founded shortly after that period
- Needs its own article

## World Research Conference
- Recurring international scientific conference (Adonian equivalent of Solvay Conferences)
- 1933 edition held in Ampuria, Volisania
- Ampuria is home to the University of Ampuria (founded 1226/1264), one of the oldest universities in Volisania
- Needs its own article

## Chemist-Physicist Dynamic
- Tân (chemist) did the radiochemical separation/identification of fission products (barium, krypton)
- Lîm (physicist) did the neutron bombardment setup, energy calculations, chain reaction theory
- Mirrors real-world fission discovery: Otto Hahn (chemist) identified barium through radiochemical analysis; Lise Meitner and Otto Frisch (physicists) provided the theoretical interpretation

## Worldbuilding Context
- **Anping**: Zong's second colony (founded 1474), modern-day Louyang. Emperor Tianhao's court-in-exile was here during the Yuanlong Restoration (c. 1585–1589).
- **Louyang**: Zong commonwealth member country in the New World (Lurandia)
- **Great Adonian War**: 1935–1947 — starts just 2 years after publication, giving fission discovery immediate strategic significance
- **Second War of the Isthmus**: 1893–1899 — Tân served 1898–1899 (tail end). Zong captured Sant Cristòfor. Treaty of San Lázaro (1899).
- **Balisca**: Uranium source for the Anping experiments (Balisca is a major uranium producer in modern lore — supplies Balboa's nuclear plant)

## Zong Language Term
- "Nuclear fission" in Zong: 核分裂 (he̍k-hun-lî) — he̍k (核, nucleus) + hun-lî (分裂, division)
