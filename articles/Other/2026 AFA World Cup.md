The '''2026 AFA World Cup''' is the 18th [[AFA World Cup]], the quadrennial international football tournament for men's senior national teams organised by the [[Adonian Football Association]] (AFA). The tournament is being co-hosted by [[Balisca]] and [[Sanrobonia]] from 14 June to 15 July 2026.

The competition features 36 teams divided into twelve groups of three. The top two teams from each group advance to the knockout stage, with the eight best group winners receiving byes to the round of 16.

The tournament is being played across twelve venues in twelve cities, split between the two host nations.

== Format ==

The 36 teams were divided into twelve groups of three. Each group was played as a single round-robin, with the top two teams in each group advancing to the knockout stage. The eight best group winners received byes to the round of 16, while the remaining four group winners and all twelve runners-up competed in a preliminary knockout round. The competition then continued through the round of 16, quarter-finals, semi-finals, a third-place match, and the final.

== Matchday 5 results ==

=== Zong 1–1 Kaneda ===
[[Kaneda national football team|Kaneda]] struck first in [[Castejón]], but [[Zong national football team|Zong]] responded late to salvage a point in a tight Group A contest.

=== Mercia 0–1 Kalisongo ===
[[Kalisongo national football team|Kalisongo]] grabbed a narrow away win against [[Mercia national football team|Mercia]] in [[Macaio]], with a second-half goal proving decisive.

=== Hersatia 2–0 Dagit ===
[[Hersatia national football team|Hersatia]] controlled the game in [[Alessandria]] and converted their superiority into a comfortable two-goal victory over [[Dagit national football team|Dagit]].

=== Galwa 3–0 Tamazgha ===
[[Galwa national football team|Galwa]] delivered the most emphatic result of the day in [[Martapura]], scoring early and adding two more after the break to beat [[Tamazgha national football team|Tamazgha]].

=== Bayan 2–2 Sanyan ===
[[Bayan national football team|Bayan]] and [[Sanyan national football team|Sanyan]] shared a lively draw in [[Axarquía]], with both teams finding the net twice in an open match.

=== Tondano 4–0 Nakamizu ===
[[Tondano national football team|Tondano]] were dominant in [[Concepción]], running out 4–0 winners against [[Nakamizu national football team|Nakamizu]].

=== Jacan 2–1 Nusaraya ===
[[Jacan national football team|Jacan]] edged [[Nusaraya national football team|Nusaraya]] in [[Castramara]] after taking control in the second half and holding on late.

=== United Midlands 0–0 Yanbaru ===
[[United Midlands national football team|United Midlands]] and [[Yanbaru national football team|Yanbaru]] could not be separated in [[Sanropura]], finishing level after a cautious, evenly balanced match.

== Summary ==

Matchday 5 produced 15 goals, highlighted by [[Tondano national football team|Tondano]]'s 4–0 win over [[Nakamizu national football team|Nakamizu]] and [[Galwa national football team|Galwa]]'s 3–0 victory over [[Tamazgha national football team|Tamazgha]]. [[Hersatia national football team|Hersatia]], [[Kalisongo national football team|Kalisongo]], and [[Jacan national football team|Jacan]] also secured important wins, while [[Bayan national football team|Bayan]] and [[Sanyan national football team|Sanyan]] shared the points.
