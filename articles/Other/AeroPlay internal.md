# AeroPlay — Internal Notes

## Overview
- Seat-centric IFE control platform by Aviònica Internacional de Ampuria (Ampuria, Volisania)
- Replaces armrest touch controller with passenger's own mobile device via companion app
- Seat-mounted touch panel retained as backup
- Launch customer: Aeroístmus — debuted June 2026 on first A330-900 (BL-WBG), all three classes, SCI–Macaio route

## Key People (invented 2026-09-14)
- **Pau Badia** — founder of Aviònica Internacional de Ampuria (2021), avionics engineer
- **Oriol Badia** — father of Pau; CEO of Volisanian Airlines
- Note: Volisanian Airlines has no article yet (only referenced at SCI/SBT airport articles and the defunct Volisanian Balboa Airlines). The CEO is mentioned here first; a Volisanian Airlines article would need creating separately.

## Product Details
- Unified app interface: seat position/recline, lighting, IFE browsing/playback (mirrored to seat-back display), crew call, moving map, menu/service requests (airline-optional module)
- Requires only cabin wireless network + certified seat control interface (supplied to seat manufacturers) — no seat modification beyond standard
- Development 2021–2025; certification trials on a Volisanian Airlines test aircraft
- Languages at launch: Volisanian, Baliscano, Mercian, Burawi, Zong
