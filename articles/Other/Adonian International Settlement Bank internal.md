# Adonian International Settlement Bank — Internal Notes

## Confirmed by user
- **Name**: Adonian International Settlement Bank (AISB)
- **Type**: International financial institution run by the Adonian Community
- **Location**: Areza
- **Established**: March 17, 1954
- **Relationship to AMA**: Separate institutions with different roles. AISB handles interbank settlement/clearing; AMA handles monetary policy/currency issuance.
- **Primary function**: BIS-like — central bank for central banks, facilitates inter-state settlements, provides banking services to AC institutions, monitors financial stability.
- **Governance**: Governor + Board of Directors (central bank governors from member states). Governor appointed by Adonian Assembly.
- **Currency role**: Handles both International dollar and national currency settlements — clearing hub for all AC financial transactions.
- **File location**: articles/Other/
