# Isthmus War — Internal Notes

## Article Info
- **File**: `articles/Global Events/Isthmus War.mediawiki`
- **Date written**: 2026-05-21
- **Scope**: Balboa isthmus + Palpoa + Sinchew–Mercian Lurandia border (NOT the Lurandian Theater, which is separate)

## Belligerents

### Volisanian coalition
| Country | Role | Motivation | Forces |
|---------|------|------------|--------|
| Volisania | Principal military power | Irredentist (recover Sant Cristòfor/canal from 1899 loss); fascist government | ~280,000 (1935), ~420,000 (1942 peak) |
| Balboa | Co-belligerent | Irredentist; authoritarian government aligned with Volisania | Constabulary (re-equipped in violation of Treaty of Sant Llàtzer) |
| Mercian Lurandia (New Hwice) | Joined 1937 | Obligated by Mercia; wanted Palpoa | Assault on Palpoa 1937 |
| United Midlands | Joined 1937 (after ML failed at Palpoa) | Allied pressure | Reinforced Palpoa campaign |
| Breisland | Naval/logistical only | Allied with Volisania | Naval and logistical support |
| Mercia | Intelligence + Lurandian Theater | Allied with Volisania; orchestrated Palpoa deal | Intelligence units on isthmus; main forces in Lurandian Theater |

### Zong coalition
| Country | Role | Motivation | Forces |
|---------|------|------------|--------|
| Zong | Principal military power | Defending Zong Balboa territory | Garrison at Sant Cristòfor; naval forces; 1942 counter-offensive |
| Sinchew | Commonwealth constituent | Obligated to support Zong | Palpoa garrison; 1942 southern axis counter-offensive |
| Louyang | Commonwealth constituent | Obligated to support Zong; joined when United Midlands entered | Lurandian Theater forces |
| Sanrobonia | Independent ally of Zong | Longstanding alliance with Zong | Naval staging; northern axis of 1942 counter-offensive |

## Key Framing
- **War fought by former colonies for former colonizers**: Sinchew/Louyang obligated as commonwealth constituents; Mercian Lurandia obligated by Mercia; United Midlands joined under allied pressure; Sanrobonia independent ally
- This is the THIRD isthmus war (after First 1711–1716, Second 1893–1899)
- Part of the Great Adonian War (1935–1947)

## Geography
- **Isthmus of Balboa**: Connects Lurandia (north) and Abyala (south). Sant Cristòfor = Kendriyan (west) coast. Sant Bart = interior capital. Canal zone runs east–west.
- **Palpoa Province**: Sinchew-owned strip south of Balboa, north of Mercian Lurandia. Mountainous terrain. Changed hands multiple times.
- **Sanrobonia**: Northern island country in Abyala. Provided naval staging and ground forces for northern axis of 1942 counter-offensive.
- **Great Lurandian Range**: Border mountain range dividing Sinchew+Louyang from Mercian Lurandia+United Midlands (Lurandian Theater — separate article).
