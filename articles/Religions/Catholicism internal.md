Created: 2026-09-04

## Purpose
Umbrella article on Catholicism in Adonia, synthesizing all existing Catholic-related lore across the wiki and explaining its branches. First article in the `articles/Religions/` folder.

## User-confirmed decisions (2026-09-04)
1. **Title**: "Catholicism" (religion-infobox article on the broader communion)
2. **Zamarra location**: In **Kastruneto** (city on Lake Zamarra) — the Illypnia article's "Zamarra, Sarta" was WRONG and has been corrected
3. **Schism status**: Great Catholic Schism (1313) is **still unresolved** — two popes, two separate communions (Petran and Zamarran) into the present day
4. **Petran Pope's seat**: The **Papal States is its own country next to Volisania** (not a city within Volisania)
5. **Sabia religion** (clarified 2026-09-04, three rounds): Sabia has both Catholics and non-Catholic Christians. Sabin Christians are descendants of settlers who emigrated from **Sarta** (~438), so the **Catholic Sabians are Petran Catholics** (tradition of their Sartan homeland), under an episcopal hierarchy separate from the BCC — approximately **18% of Sabia** (this is Sabian Catholic/Petran adherence, NOT BCC; the BCC demographics table's 18% row for Sabia is thus a mislabel/conflation — Sabia should arguably be removed or footnoted in the BCC article itself). The non-Catholic Christian Sabians belong to a tradition outside the Catholic communion.

## Branch structure (as presented)
1. **Petran Catholic Church** — Pope in the Papal States (own country next to Volisania). States: Volisania, Sarta, Jacan, Potocsí (71.2%), Balboa (~40%), Balisca (10%), Guajicutea (66%). BCC in communion.
2. **Zamarran Catholic** — Pope at Zamarra, Kastruneto. States: Kastruneto, Breisland, Nabia. Minority in southern Sarta.
3. **Burawi Catholic Church** — 573, autonomous, in communion with Petran, Cardinal of Burawa at Weli Markos, ~115M adherents, Burawi rite.
4. **Former**: Lacashe Church (1789–early 1890s).
