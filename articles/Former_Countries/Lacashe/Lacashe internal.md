## Three Historical Phases

### Phase 1: Post-Independence Slave Society (1789–1856)
- Cotton exports grew, became one of world's largest exporters
- Cotton gin adoption increased profitability
- Alliances: Breisland, Kingdom of Zong
- Complex relationship with Burawa: cut political ties but still imported cotton
- Slave trade evolution:
  - Burawa banned slave trade → turned to Volisania
  - 1815: slave trade began sustained decline
  - 1837: Balisca anti-slavery campaign accelerated decline
  - 1842: Volisania abolished slave trade
  - 1843: Breisland abolished slave trade
  - Cut off from imports, but most slaves by then were native-born
  - 1848: Balboa abolished slavery → Lacashe last New World slave state
- Embargoes (1837–1856): Burawa, Volisania, Balisca, Breisland, Zong, Moros, others
- Only ally: Potocsi (western neighbour)
- 1851: Lacashe paid cotton smugglers to flood markets
- Constant slave revolts, maroon communities
- Legal repression: freedmen excluded from property, deportations of non-Burawi groups
- Population exploded due to wealth
- **Lacashe-Asikyira War (Mountain War)** 1838–1840: Invaded newly independent Bindorama/Asikyira, gained ~5,000 km² in mountains for gold access
- **War of Liberation (1853–1856)**: Coalition of Burawa, Volisania, Balisca, Asikyira + internal slave rebellions

### Phase 2: Post-War of Liberation (1856–1935)
- **Partition**: Nation split in two
  - **East coast**: Libertalia (freedman state, led by Marcus Galvan and Freedman Congress)
  - **West/interior**: Remaining Republic of Lacashe
- **Bloody Decade (1856–1866)**: Forced migrations, lynchings, occupation
- Foreign military occupation until 1863 (except Porto Vren border city, free port)
- Treaty terms:
  - Balisca got cotton export control rights until 1873
  - 30 million hanti concessions to Lacashian state
  - 75 million hanti reparations from planters to Libertalia
- **Trustees** period: Moderates led by Eduardo Vitalis
- **Lacashe Freedom Society**: Extremist paramilitary emerged
- **Lacashe Citizen's Movement**: Political wing of Freedom Society
- 1863: First non-controlled election → Citizen's Movement won
- **Libertad-Lacashe War (Reconquest)** 1875–1880: Lacashe invaded, pushed back by Asikyiran army
- 1889: Libertalia merged with Asikyira
- Population collapse: Lost ~80% of peak population
- Failed state period: Limited admin capacity outside Guledga
- Attempted industrialization with limited success

### Phase 3: Modern Lacashe (1935–present)
- **Great Adonian War (1935–1947)**: Lacashe neutral, supplied cotton and foodstuffs to combatants, economic recovery
- **Blood History Law (1884)**: Formal classification of Lacashocho ("good heritage", Burawi/Volisanian, lighter-skinned) vs Lacashito ("bad heritage", Kaftian/Ayorin, darker-skinned)
- **Separate Origins Acts (1930)**: Codified segregation in all spheres except factory labour
- 1880–1930: Informal segregation, violence against Lacashito
- **State capitalism (1920s+)**: Former planters became political class, owned businesses through state
- **1950 reforms**: Economic depression + sanctions + AC denial forced civil rights reforms
- 1950s–1960s: Communist insurgencies (Lacashe Kadarist Association) funded by Burawa/Balisca
- 1960s–1980s: Domestic unrest, repressive government responses
- **Guladga University Massacre (1981)**: Mass arrests of students
- 1980–1982: Violent domestic conflict
- **AC intervention**: Adonian Community forces activated by Council
- **1983 constitution**: Universal suffrage (previously landowning men only)
- First election: New Liberal Party won, Socialist Party second, Old Guard Party third
- Modern population: 89% Lacashocho, 11% Lacashito (due to emigration)
- Contemporary: Stable, modest standard of living, high inequality, insular society
