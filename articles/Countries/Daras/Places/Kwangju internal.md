# Kwangju internal notes

## Overview
Socialist autonomous region in southwestern Daras. Korean-inspired (IRL: Korea). Capital: Jinju. Area: 184,240 km². Population: 22,054,000.

## Real-world inspiration
- **Kwangjuan people** = Korean ethnic group
- **Kwangeo language** = Korean language
- **Darasian** = Mandarin Chinese
- **Kingdom of Seorabeol** = inspired by Silla (Seorabeol was the old name for Silla's capital)
- **Climate** = Manchuria-like, temperate, cold winters
- **Terrain** = Northern mountains, central hills, rugged southern coast with peninsulas
- **Economic model** = Rust Belt / Manchurian industrial decline and recovery

## Key facts
- Borders: Lamudalai (W), Nanshan Province (N), Bayanqing Province (E), Southern Ocean (S)
- Languages: Kwangeo (official, primary), Darasian (compulsory secondary, most people bilingual)
- Writing system: Garimgeul (native phonemic script), Darasian script (secondary)
- Religion: ~52% Buddhist, ~38% non-religious, ~10% other
- Ethnicity: ~78% Kwangjuan, ~18% Darasian, ~4% other
- Jinju population: ~10 million
- Economy: middle-income, heavy industry + manufacturing + refining, some non-industrial growth
- Jinju: port city, heavy industry, shipbuilding, steel, manufacturing, automotive
- Kwangju = one of the most polluted regions in Daras
- Population growth: above Darasian average, larger families, younger demographic profile

## Garimgeul script
- Created in 16th century by court philosopher **Bak Seong-rin** (박성린)
- Garimgeul = 가림 (garim, "to distinguish/separate") + 글 (geul, "writing/script")
- Purpose: both practical (literacy for common people) and cultural (distinguish Kwangjuan identity from Darasian and Lamudalai)
- Replaced Darasian characters for domestic use; Darasian characters remained for diplomatic/formal correspondence
- Spread first through Buddhist monasteries, then merchants and local officials
- Darasian court viewed it with suspicion (political separatism); Seorabeol king defended it as practical
- IRL inspiration: Hangul (created 1443 by King Sejong); adapted for worldbuilding

## Economic history arc
1. **Pre-1840s**: Agrarian tributary kingdom, modest economy
2. **1840s–1904**: Coal boom — vast coal deposits discovered in northern mountains, private Darasian mining concerns operate under Seorabeol court licences, region becomes wealthy extractive state selling coal to Darasian industrial core, infrastructure investment (roads, Jinju harbour), demographic shift (Darasian workers arrive), dependency on Darasian demand
3. **1911–c. 1940**: Post-revolutionary depression — private mining nationalised and dissolved, oil transition reduces coal demand, patronage networks collapse, unemployment rises, out-migration of young Kwangjuans to northern cities, limited state investment in southwest
4. **c. 1940–present**: Industrial recovery — Daras directs investment to heavy industry in Kwangju (shipbuilding, steel, automotive), region positioned as finishing centre (raw/partial goods from north → finished/ assembled in Kwangju → exported via Jinju to Nakamizu, Okaiken, Lurandia), middle-income recovery, severe pollution from heavy industry, recent green transition efforts (electric vehicles, renewables, emissions controls)

## History
- 924: Kingdom of Seorabeol capitulated to Darasian imperial forces — last holdout of southern expansion
- 924–1906: Tributary period — nominal independence, own royal house, internal administration, tribute to Daras
- c. 1840s: Coal boom begins — private Darasian mining concerns, Seorabeol becomes wealthy extractive state
- 1904–1905: Darasian Socialist Revolution — King Hyeonjong negotiated neutrality with Chen Lixian; Seorabeol stayed out of the war
- 1906: Incorporated into Socialist Republic of Daras as autonomous region, still under King Hyeonjong (subordinate to People's Party)
- 1911: Hyeonjong died. Heavenly Emperor's People's Army entered Kwangju to manage transition. Hyeonjong posthumously made "Ally of the Revolution" and "Second Heavenly King" (honorific). Region converted to autonomous republic with own parliament, overseen by Socialist Council liaison.
- 1911–c. 1940: Post-revolutionary depression — nationalisation, oil transition, coal decline, out-migration
- c. 1940–present: Heavy industry recovery — shipbuilding, automotive, steel, finishing centre role

## Political structure
- Unitary parliamentary autonomous republic
- Parliament of Kwangju: unicameral, handles domestic policy
- Socialist Council of Daras liaison: oversight, rarely vetoes
- Parties: Socialist Unity Party (largest), General People's Party
- Kwangjuan-specific political movements exist within socialist framework

## Geography
- Northern mountains (border with Nanshan) — coal seams, mineral deposits
- Central rolling hills and upland plateaus
- Southern coast: numerous peninsulas, rugged headlands, natural harbours
- Rivers flow south from highlands to Southern Ocean
- Climate: temperate, cold winters, moderate summers, snow in northern mountains
- NOTE: Southern hemisphere — corrected from "northerly latitudes" to "southerly latitudes"
