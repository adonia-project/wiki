# Great Zhongyuan — Internal Notes

## Purpose
Create a standalone article for **Great Zhongyuan**, the peninsula-wide imperial state that followed the fall of Donghai and preceded the successor kingdoms.

## User guidance incorporated
- The user wants to work on Great Zhongyuan before moving on to other successor states.
- The capital should be **invented** rather than defaulting to Zhongyuan itself.
- This article should complement, not duplicate, the **Eternal Zongic Wars** article.

## Working interpretation
- Great Zhongyuan is the high imperial phase of the Zongic conflict cycle.
- It begins after Donghai falls in 285 CE.
- It is founded and dominated by **King Yuanxiong of Zhongyuan**.
- Its collapse is caused by the **War of the Four Sons** after Yuanxiong's death in 300 CE.
- The successor kingdoms are **Haizhou**, **Nanyang**, **Jiaozhi**, and **Zhongyuan**.

## Invented details used
- Capital: **Zhongjing**
- Central royal capital chosen to sound imperial and peninsula-wide.
- This gives Great Zhongyuan a distinct identity from the older inland kingdom of Zhongyuan.
- Geography: Great Zhongyuan occupied the lands north of the Huo River, with Chiang State to the south and Beilong excluded from the imperial core in the northwest.
