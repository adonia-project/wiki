# List of emperors of Zong — Internal Notes

## Purpose
Create a chronological list article for the imperial rulers of Zong, focused on the early imperial period after the proclamation of the Jade Empire.

## User guidance incorporated
- The user wants the list article to be **imperial only**.
- The list should be a **chronological table**.
- For now, the article should focus on the **early imperial period**.
- The article should connect to the **Golden Age of Zong**.

## Working interpretation
The list article is meant to support the Golden Age era article by giving readers a compact chronology of the first imperial rulers. It begins after the unification era and shows how the same House of Tian continued into the assembly-based court state.

## Rulers used
- **Emperor Tianwu of Zong** — reform ruler, central adoption of the Societal Assembly
- **Emperor Tianhe of Zong** — successor emperor, continued court-state consolidation

## Article structure used
1. Short lead paragraph
2. Chronological table
3. See also
4. References

## Tone and style notes
- Keep the article concise and utilitarian.
- Treat the list as a reference page rather than a narrative history article.
- Make the connection to the Golden Age clear but do not duplicate the full era narrative.

## Open questions for future work
- Whether later emperors should be added as their names are developed.
- Whether the list should eventually be split into dynasty sub-sections once the imperial chronology becomes longer.
