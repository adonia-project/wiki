# Eternal Zongic Wars — Internal Notes

## Purpose
Create an era article for the long Zongic conflict cycle spanning roughly **150 BCE to 350 CE**. The article should connect the Zhongyuan-Donghai Wars, the fall of Donghai, the creation of Great Zhongyuan, and the War of the Four Sons.

## User corrections incorporated
- The era should begin around **150 BCE**, not 200 CE.
- The article should use **Zhongyuan** consistently.
- The 894 Great Peace material belongs to the later Zong unification article, not this one.
- The succession crisis must include the **War of the Four Sons**.

## Working interpretation
- **Han**, **Zhao**, and **Qi** are absorbed into Zhongyuan during the early expansion phase.
- **Donghai** remains the coastal rival until 285 CE.
- **Great Zhongyuan** is the reunited imperial state after Donghai's fall.
- **King Yuanxiong of Zhongyuan** is the great king who rules Great Zhongyuan before the succession crisis.
- The four sons are working names for the claimant princes who generate the successor kingdoms.
- The era also overlaps with the **Zongyuan-Miyagi War**, which leads to the founding of [[Jingū Minato]] as a colonial foothold and its loss around 100 CE.
