# Baiduist Expulsion from Zong — internal notes

## Overview
1606 decree by Emperor Tianxiao and the Societal Assembly to ban Baiduism, expel foreign Baiduists, and relocate Zong Baiduists to Sinchew colony. Led to the 1606-1623 Baidu People's Rebellion (100,000+ killed, genocide characterization). By 1650, over 1M Baidu Zong people moved to Sinchew.

## Key names
- **Emperor**: Tianxiao of Zong (天孝) — "Heavenly Filial Piety", reigned 1598–1643
- **Decree**: 1606, promulgated by Societal Assembly
- **Colony**: Sinchew, established 1538 at Tamsui (penal/plantation colony)
- **Rebellion**: Baidu People's Rebellion (1606–1623)

## Timeline
- 1538: Sinchew colony established at Tamsui
- 1500s: Escalating Tianist-Baiduist tensions
- 1606: Decree promulgated — ban on Baiduism, expulsion of foreign Baiduists, relocation of Zong Baiduists to Sinchew
- 1606–1623: Baidu People's Rebellion — informal Baiduist uprisings, 100,000+ killed
- 1623: Rebellion effectively suppressed
- 1650: Over 1M Zong Baiduists relocated to Sinchew

## Religious context
- **Baiduism** replaces Buddhism in Adonia (user confirmed 2026-06-14)
- Baiduism in Zong: primarily eastern coast, maritime trading communities
- Tianism: state religion, ordains emperor's legitimacy, dominant in west and capital
- Golden Age policy: religious pluralism (Tianism at court, Baiduism tolerated in south/east)
- Post-expulsion: Baiduism eliminated as significant force in Zong

## Foreign Baiduists expelled
- Mixed: Darasian, Shansian, and other continental Baiduist merchants/monks
- Perceived as potential agents of Darasian influence (post-Jade Sea War suspicion)
- Returned to countries of origin or relocated to other Baiduist territories

## Sinchew colony
- Established 1538 at Tamsui
- Initially penal colony and plantation settlement
- Hot, humid, disease-ridden — "make fortunes or die"
- Baiduist influx transformed it into major population centre
- Zong Baiduist tradition (Ocean Crossing emphasis) well-suited to colonial environment
- Foundation for later development as major New World polity

## Genocide characterization
- Modern historians characterize as genocide
- 100,000+ killed during rebellion
- Deliberate targeting of religious population
- Destruction of religious institutions
- Forced displacement of entire religious population
