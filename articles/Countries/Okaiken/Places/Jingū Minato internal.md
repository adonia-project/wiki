# Jingū Minato — Internal Notes

## Purpose
Create a standalone article for **Jingū Minato**, the Sanese mercantile colony established on the eastern Jade Peninsula during the reign of Empress Jingū.

## User-provided geographic detail
- The colony is **east of Jiaozhi**.
- The colony is **southeast of Haizhou**.
- It is separated from the inland territories by the **Rìluò range**.
- The coastal district had previously owed allegiance to the **Qi state**.

## Working interpretation
- Jingū Minato was founded after the **Zongyuan-Miyagi War**.
- It functioned as both a **commercial foothold** and a **strategic buffer**.
- It was lost around **100 CE** during the **Zongyuan-Miyagi War**, within the broader **Eternal Zongic Wars**.
- The article intentionally avoids inventing a more specific indigenous district name, because one has not been established.
