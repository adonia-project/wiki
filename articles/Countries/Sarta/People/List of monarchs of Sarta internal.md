# List of monarchs of Sarta — Internal Notes

## Purpose
Create a reference list for the monarchs of Sarta from the 1197 unification onward. The article should stay short and utilitarian, with the chronology handled as a table.

## Working interpretation
- Sarta follows hereditary primogeniture.
- The royal house is the [[House of Fuertelon]].
- [[Ramiro I of Fuertelon|Ramiro I]] is the first king of unified Sarta.
- The list article is a companion to [[History of Sarta (1197-1313)]].

## Article structure
1. Short lead paragraph
2. Chronological table
3. See also
4. Categories

## Known rulers
- [[Ramiro I of Fuertelon|Ramiro I]] — first king; founder of the unified kingdom.
- [[Sancho I of Sarta|Sancho I]] — consolidated the southern conquests and began the Tamarin war.
- [[Fernando I of Sarta|Fernando I]] — secured the mountain frontier, annexed Tamarin, and settled the Jacan treaty.
- [[Alfonso I of Sarta|Alfonso I]] — ruled through the buildup to the Great Catholic Schism.
