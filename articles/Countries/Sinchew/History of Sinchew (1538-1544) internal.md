| **20 Nov 1543** | **CKG arrested at Tamsui** — *confirmed by the user, 2026-09-21* |

| 1544 | court receives two irreconcilable accounts; **sends its own investigators** |
| **1 Jun 1544** | **royal edict issued at Huwei** — deliberated in the [[Societal Assembly]], approved by [[Tiancheng of Zong|Emperor Tiancheng]] |

**The two-date structure of the edict is the best detail in the article:** *"the colony was accordingly divided in law from June and in fact from November, and the same promulgation that constituted the Sinchew Colony released the man it had been sent to depose."*

## 4. What the article is really about
**The shortest period in the territory's history and the most consequential.** Six years in which a trading outpost at a bay became the capital of a crown colony, and the peoples of the coast were destroyed or reduced to clients of the settlement built among them.

**And the bitter irony at the centre:** CKG ruled Okheng as a sovereign with direct trade. He gave that up in 1535 to become a colonial administrator — **and within seven years he was worse supplied than he had been as a private king**, which is why he broke the colony's trade rules. He was then arrested, accused of trying to restore the polity he had been forced to sell, held for a year, and released on the day the colony was founded. **The edict that freed him created the state he had asked for and did not get.**
