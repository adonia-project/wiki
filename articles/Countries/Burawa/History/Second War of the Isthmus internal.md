# Second War of the Isthmus — Internal Notes

## Article Overview
- **File**: `Second War of the Isthmus.mediawiki`
- **Subject**: Zongese–Sinchewese–Louyangese victory over Volisanian–Balboan–Burawi coalition for control of the Balboa isthmus
- **Period**: 1893–1899
- **Scope**: Full background (Balboa's post-slavery transition, Balboa Canal, Zongosphere immigration, Illypnian anxieties), war course, Treaty of San Lázaro, and aftermath connecting to Great Adonian War

## Core Facts
- **Triggered by**: Balboa impressing two Zongese vessels that refused to pay canal transit fees (1893)
- **Deeper causes**: Balboa Canal ownership (not subject to Treaty of Remoria), Zong's refusal to pay fees, Volisania's desire to build international coalition against Zong
- **Ended by**: Treaty of San Lázaro (1899)
- **Key outcome**: Zong gets Sant Cristòfor + Balboa Canal; Sinchew gets southern Balboan strip; Treaty of Remoria voided

## Belligerents
### Zongese coalition
- **Zong**: Primary attacker, naval power, captured Sant Cristòfor in 1893
- **Sinchew**: Commonwealth realm of Zong, invaded southern Balboa by land (22,000 troops)
- **Louyang**: Commonwealth realm of Zong, naval support (5,000)

### Illypnian coalition
- **Volisania**: Primary defender, naval power, provided military support to Balboa
- **Balboa**: The country being fought over, started the war by impressing Zongese vessels
- **Burawa**: ~15,000 ground forces, bound by alliance to Volisania
- **Lacashe**: ~4,000 forces
- **Potocsí**: ~3,000 forces
- **Galwa**: ~2,000 forces

## Key Dates
- **1848**: Slavery abolished in Balboa (still Volisanian colony)
- **1854**: Balboa gains independence from Volisania
- **1870s**: Balboa Canal construction begins (Volisanian-funded)
- **1885**: Balboa Canal completed
- **1893**: Zongese vessels impressed by Balboa → skirmish → war declared; Zong captures Sant Cristòfor
- **1894**: Sinchewese invasion of southern Balboa opens land front
- **1894–1896**: Naval stalemate, land fighting in southern Balboa
- **1897–1899**: Coalition exhaustion, negotiations
- **1899**: Treaty of San Lázaro signed

## Background Details (from user)
- Balboa was second-to-last Volisanian colony to abolish slavery (1848)
- Post-slavery: most freed slaves continued on plantations as free men, conditions barely changed
- Racism still rampant, Volisania didn't enforce civil rights
- Sant Cristòfor was a cosmopolitan free port — the only economically viable city
- Influx of Zongosphere immigration (from Sinchew, Dagit, northern Fosia) into Sant Cristòfor
- Balboa's capital: Sant Bart (inland)
- Balboa Canal was deliberately structured to be owned by Balboa, not Volisania, so Treaty of Remoria passage rights didn't apply
- Zong didn't expect the canal until after 1885 completion, then discovered high fees
- Zong tried negotiations first, then just sent navy ships through without paying
- Volisania initially provided military support but realized it could use the conflict to turn international opinion against Zong
- Sinchew and Balboa share a border — Sinchew invaded from the south
