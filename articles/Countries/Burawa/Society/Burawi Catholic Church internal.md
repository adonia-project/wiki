## User Answers (2026-08-07)

### Church authority
- In communion with the **Petran Catholic Church** (the Volisanian Catholic Church)
- The Petran Catholic Church has a **pope**
- The Cardinal of Burawa is head of the Burawi church but recognises the broader spiritual authority of the Petran pope
- Self-governing internally (autocephalous in practice, in communion in doctrine)

### Theology
- **Trinitarian Catholic** — similar to Roman Catholicism
- "Catholic" means "universal" — considers itself part of the universal Christian church
- Main distinction from Lathrianism: trinitarianism vs nontrinitarianism
- Liturgy conducted in **Burawi language**

### Sabins
- **Separate tradition** — the Sabins who settled in Sabia by ~438 AD established their own Christian tradition
- They are NOT part of the Burawi Catholic Church
- They predate the formal establishment of the BCC (573 AD)
- Their tradition remains separate

## Invented Details (subject to user review)

### Monastic Orders
1. **Order of Saint Guled** — oldest Burawi monastic order, founded c. 750, contemplative and scholarly, manuscript preservation, theological scholarship, produced "On the Dignity of All Peoples" (1700), scriptoria, moderate asceticism, intellectual tradition
2. **Order of the Holy Apostles** — founded c. 680s at Kaysoor, liturgical and contemplative, associated with Cathedral of the Holy Apostles, liturgical music tradition, pastoral and sacramental ministry
3. **Bartay Society** (1611) — fraternal order modelled on Jesuits, evangelisation/education/charity, extensive school network

### Saints
- Saint Guled — early Kaftian Christian scholar, missionary among northern peoples, c. 8th century
- Saint Iacob (Iacob I) — first Christian king, recognised as founder of the Church
- Saint Petros — early martyr, first bishop of Mazar
- Saint Aamina — early Christian woman, first convert in Mazar

### Petran Catholic Church
- The Volisanian Catholic Church is called the **Petran Catholic Church**
- It has a pope (presumably in Volisania, possibly in a city named after Peter/Petros)
- The Burawi Catholic Church is in communion with this church
- This is the broader Catholic communion that the BCC belongs to

### Structure
- Cardinal of Burawa: head of the BCC, seat at Weli Markos (after 1709)
- Archdioceses: Samara, Mazar, Iskhal, Badalga, Hargiesa, Lacashe (historical)
- Dioceses: regional, corresponding to historical provinces
- Parishes: local level
