# Taxis of Burawa — Internal Notes

## Key Facts (from user)
- **All taxi drivers** are employed by administration transit agencies (state employees, not private)
- **Median salary**: 48,200–72,400 Hanti ($12,050–$18,100)
- **Pre-revolution (pre-1932)**: Cabs popular among elite and middle class; died after socialist revolution
- **1932–1964**: Informal vehicle-for-hire only
- **1964**: Vehicle Hire Act passed — allowed administrations to establish vehicle-for-hire services to supplement public transportation
- **Slow growth** post-1964; most popular in:
  - Lanwaley and Sago (less comprehensive public transit)
  - Mazar (status symbol)
- **Early taxis**: Random vehicles with just a taxi marker on top
- **1970s**: Surge of scams and kidnappings from false taxi cabs
- **1977**: Taxicab Standardization Bill passed
  - BMC 18 (sedan) and BMC MiniBus 4 (minivan) = original exclusive taxi vehicles
  - Law established 5-year review cycle; Ministry of Transportation updates standards every 5 years
  - Lime green checkered livery — iconic 70s/80s look
- **Current fleet (as of 2026)** — user-specified:
  - BMC Wiyar 2016 (sedan, ICE)
  - BMC Wiyar 2018 (sedan, electric)
  - BMC Badalga 2018 (minibus, ICE)
  - BMC Badalga 2022 (minibus, electric)
- **Mazar is part of South Hudur** — same fare structure, not a separate administration
- **South Hudur flag drop**: 4.50 Ħ (corrected from 3.50)
- **1979**: Taxiworkers Union of Burawa established
  - First ask: more workers (undersupply of hiring)
- **1990s**: Taxiing less popular as public transit improved
  - BUT tourism boom brought taxis back (fare card systems too complex for tourists)
  - Greater hiring of workers
- **2000s**: Fare card systems standardized, more info online → taxi slump
  - Further increased by rise of rideshare apps (Sare, Z2Z)
  - Private vehicles for hire not illegal but unheard of until Sare/Z2Z
- **2016**: First major taxiworkers protest (taxi hiring slowed due to ridesharing)
  - Government responded: Licensed Ride Hailing Act
  - Banned private vehicles for hire not run by state if orchestrated by third party
  - Cited safety: sexual assaults by drivers in Burawa and abroad
- **2018**: Ministry of Innovation created BuFulid app for taxi driver hailing
