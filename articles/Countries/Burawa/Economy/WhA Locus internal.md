## Key Facts (from user)
- **Name**: WhA Locus
- **Designer**: Wasaaradda Hal-abuurka (WhA) — Ministry of Innovation in Burawi
- **Manufacturer**: Burawi Motor Company (BMC) — state-owned automotive manufacturer
- **Assembly**: BMC Khar Plant 11, Khar
- **Type**: 4-door autonomous electric SUV / Mini MPV
- **Release/Service Date**: August 14, 2026
- **Autonomy**: SAE Level 4 (LiDAR + high-resolution cameras)
- **First deployment**: South Hudur Metro Transportation Authority, South Hudur Metropolitan Administration
- **Initial fleet**: 150 vehicles entered service on day one
- **Power**: 275 hp (205 kW)
- **Battery**: 95 kWh lithium-ion
- **Motor**: Permanent magnet synchronous, dual motor AWD
- **Body**: Mini MPV, angular finishes, resembles a minibus
- **Doors**: Two per side, outward-sliding
- **Development start**: 2022 (by Ministry of Innovation in collaboration with BMC)
- **Government order**: 10,000 units from Government of Burawa
- **Purpose**: Robotaxi services nationwide
- **Next rollout cities**: Kankadadka Capital Region, Badalga, Sago
- **Controversy**: August 1 announcement met with protest by Ururka Shaqaalaha Taksiga ee Ugu Weyn (principal taxi workers' union)
