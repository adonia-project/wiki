# August 2026 Taxiworkers Union Protest — Internal Notes

## Article Details
- **File**: `articles/Countries/Burawa/Events/2026 Taxiworkers Union protest.mediawiki`
- **Created**: 2026-08-14
- **Subject**: Ongoing labour protest by Taxiworkers Union of Burawa against WhA Locus robotaxi deployment

## Key Facts (from user)
- **Start date**: 1 August 2026
- **Status**: Ongoing (as of 14 August 2026)
- **Cause**: Ministry of Innovation announced beginning of WhA Locus taxi service in select locations
- **Taxiworkers**: Salaried employees of various metro administrations
- **Protest type**: Work stoppage
- **First two days**: Immediate stoppage of all taxis in Burawa, followed by union protest at Khar plant and the capital

### Previous Protest (April 2026)
- Date: 18 April 2026
- Trigger: Testing began of Locus in Khar
- Purpose: Inform people of safety concerns and possible removal of jobs
- Duration: 3-day work stoppage
- Muna Xasan promised: training would last until deemed safe, said it would take a "long time"
- Muna Xasan promised: extension of displacement fund protections to taxiworkers (existing Transportation Workers Displacement Fund from 2022 did not include taxiworkers)

### August Protest Timeline
- **Aug 1**: Work stoppage begins, all taxis halted
- **Aug 1-2**: First two days — immediate stoppage of all taxis, union protest at Khar plant and capital
- **Aug 2**: Muna Xasan press conference — asked taxiworkers to stop, negotiate union settlement and disbursement for lost jobs, called for parliament to accelerate creation of the fund
- **Aug 3**: Central government disbursed extra funds to transit authorities to increase services in affected areas
- **Aug 4-7**: Continued stoppage, government did not invoke Emergency Workforce Authorization Act 1964
- **Aug 8**: Transportation Workers Displacement Fund Addendum drafted — would amend existing 2022 fund to include taxiworkers; fully funds salaries and pensions for taxiworkers with 4+ years in industry
- **Aug 11**: Senate vote — passed 131 to 99
- **Aug 12**: Lower House vote — failed 312 to 484 (multiple party splits; see bill article)
- **Aug 14**: Protest ongoing

### Political Context
- Muna Xasan = Leader of the Burawi Republic, Party for Equitable Progress (PEP)
- PEP has 22 Lower House seats, 0 Senate seats
- Xasan is an outsider politician with limited parliamentary support
- Senate: 230 seats (131-99 vote = 230 total, simple majority = 116, passed with 131)
- Lower House: 796 seats (312-484 vote = 796 total, simple majority = 399, failed with 312)
- Socialist Party split on the Addendum: budget-conscious (too expensive), Industrial-aligned (just reassign to state jobs), supportive faction
- Industrial Party: opposed outright — taxiworkers can get new state jobs via reworking programme
- Forward Party: proposed retraining programmes instead of salary/pension funding
- Communist Party / further-left: bill not expansive enough — should cover ALL taxiworkers regardless of tenure
- National Party: split between budget conservatives and union appeasers
- CSU: supports taxiworkers' position

### Emergency Workforce Authorization Act 1964
- Permits government to redirect resources/personnel to maintain essential services in place of striking workers
- Xasan chose NOT to invoke it — committed to establishing displacement fund instead
- Political sensitivity: deploying emergency powers against salaried public-sector workers
