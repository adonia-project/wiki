# Burawa Open — Internal Notes

## Overview
The Burawa Open is Burawa's annual major tennis tournament. It is held in Samara at the National Tennis Center in the first half of July and is commonly called the Samara Open.

## Agreed source-of-truth details
- **Official name:** Burawa Open
- **Burawi name:** Furitaanka Burawa (from *fur* = "to open" + *-itaan* = noun-forming suffix + *-ka* = definite article; "The Opening of Burawa")
- **Common nickname:** Samara Open
- **Location:** Samara, at the National Tennis Center
- **Capital status in 1893:** Samara was the capital
- **Surface:** Hard court
- **Founded:** 1893
- **Editions:** 117 (as of 2026; no editions held during Burawi Domestic War 1932–1935 and Great Adonian War 1935–1948)
- **Timing:** First half of July each year
- **Status:** Major open tournament in Burawa
- **AWTO relationship:** Affiliated with the Adonian World Tennis Organization, but not officially part of a circuit
- **Role in calendar:** Warm-up event for the Grand Slam season, which begins roughly a month later
- **Duration:** 14 days
- **Entry structure:** Open preliminaries for all nationalities; main-draw access requires a minimum AWTO ranking
- **Events:** Men’s, women’s, and mixed singles and doubles
- **Trophy:** Open Shield
- **Prize money:** $10,000,000
- **Tradition:** Local music week
- **Funding history:** Originally part of the Gentlemen's League (1911–1930); withdrew amid Volisanian-Burawi tensions; operated independently after the Great Adonian War under Ministry of Humanity funding
- **Current funding:** Government-funded; ticket sales, merchandise, and international broadcasting revenue; no sponsorships
