# Transportation Workers Displacement Fund Addendum — Internal Notes

## Article Details
- **File**: `articles/Countries/Burawa/Politics/Transportation Workers Displacement Fund Addendum.mediawiki`
- **Created**: 2026-08-14
- **Subject**: Failed bill to amend the Transportation Workers Displacement Fund to include taxiworkers

## Key Facts (from user)
- **Transportation Workers Displacement Fund**: Established 2022 specifically for passenger train drivers facing a legislated phase-out by 2035 (automated trains). Did NOT include taxiworkers. Fund was designed for a defined, time-bound automation programme with a clear displacement horizon.
- **Bill name**: Transportation Workers Displacement Fund Addendum
- **Introduced**: 8 August 2026
- **Provisions**: Fully fund salaries and pensions for taxiworkers with at least 4 years in the industry
- **Senate vote**: 11 August — passed 131 to 99 (230 total seats)
- **Lower House vote**: 12 August — failed 312 to 484 (796 total seats)

## Key Argument Against Bill
- The government does not intend to reduce taxiworker employment anytime soon
- Initial Locus deployment = 150 vehicles in one metro area, no broader phase-out announced
- Adding taxiworkers to the fund would expand obligations without a comparable displacement timeline
- Contrast with train drivers: explicit phase-out by 2035 for automated trains — fund was designed for this specific, time-bound transition
