# Transportation Workers Displacement Fund Act — Internal Notes

## Article Details
- **File**: `articles/Countries/Burawa/Politics/Transportation Workers Displacement Fund.mediawiki`
- **Created**: 2026-08-14
- **Subject**: Original 2022 legislation establishing the Transportation Workers Displacement Fund

## Key Facts (from user)
- **Established**: 2022
- **Purpose**: Fund for all positions explicitly going to be phased out due to automation
- **Eligibility window**: Workers employed between 20 years before AND at least 4 years from a planned phase-out date
- **Initial coverage**: Passenger rail operators only
- **Phase-out date for rail**: 2035 (automated trains)
- **Amendment mechanism**: Bill noted it could be amended to include new roles in the transportation sector
- **This amendment mechanism is what enabled the Transportation Workers Displacement Fund Addendum (Aug 2026)**

## Eligibility Window Logic
- 20-year lookback: covers workers with long tenure in affected positions
- 4-year minimum: excludes workers who joined shortly before phase-out was announced (they entered with knowledge of impending automation)
- For passenger rail (phase-out 2035): eligibility window = employed 2002–2031

## Relationship to Addendum
- The Addendum (Aug 2026) tried to add taxiworkers using the amendment mechanism
- Key opposition argument: no defined phase-out timeline for taxiworkers (unlike rail's 2035 deadline)
- The fund's design principle: coverage tied to planned, time-bound automation programmes
- Adding taxiworkers would break this principle — no displacement horizon

  - 14 Jun: Committee reported without amendment
  - 28 Jun: Senate debate

- **Fund registration**: Began September 2022
