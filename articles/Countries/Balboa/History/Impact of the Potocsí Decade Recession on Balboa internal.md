# Impact of the Potocsí Decade Recession on Balboa — Internal Notes

## Key Facts
- **Event**: Potocsí Decade Recession (1977–1987) — triggered by Camboriú War + Marañón oil fires
- **Impact on Balboa**: Four channels — oil supply, trade/canal, refugees, asset acquisition
- **Article type**: Standalone + cross-referenced in History of Balboa (1973–1993)

## Four Impact Channels

### 1. Oil Supply Disruption
- Petrolí de Balboa (PB, pre-CIEA) was purely downstream — imported crude, refined at Badia Curta
- Potocsí's Marañón basin was a significant crude source
- Marañón oil fires (Nov 1976 – Apr 1977) destroyed 612 wells, 105 tanks, 6 facilities
- Combined with global oil shock → higher crude prices for PB
- PB forced to find alternative suppliers (Ayorin, Sanyan, Kaftian, Illypnian)
- Higher fuel costs → transport, industry, agriculture (cooperative farms dependent on diesel)
- Government couldn't subsidize (already in 1968-1978 recession)
- Informed later policy: 1996 PB+SMSS merger giving upstream production

### 2. Trade/Canal Revenue
- Potocsian oil exports had been significant northbound canal traffic → eliminated
- Potocsí GDP -35% → reduced demand for imports through canal
- SCB (Serveis del Canal de Balboa) saw reduced transit volumes
- Partially offset by global oil shock increasing value of other oil shipments
- Sant Cristòfor Free Trade Zone (1978) partly a response — diversify trade partners

### 3. Refugees
- ~800,000 IDPs in Potocsí; portion sought refuge in Balboa
- Entry via Tramuntana land border + maritime routes to Sant Cristòfor
- Settled in working-class ciutats: Sant Llorenç, Ciutat de l'Estació
- No formal refugee policy — ad hoc status, uncertain legal position
- Strain on housing/social services in already depressed economy
- Spanish-speaking → some linguistic integration with Volisanian-speaking coast
- Formal framework not enacted until 1990s

### 4. TEB Acquisition (1984)
- Comunicacions Potocsianes → TEB for $180M
- Controversial in Potocsí (selling national assets to foreigners)
- Strategic success for TEB — 2M subscribers, later 50M by 2026
- Part of broader pattern of Balboan companies buying distressed Potocsian assets
- Facilitated by overvalued ID (purchasing power advantage)
