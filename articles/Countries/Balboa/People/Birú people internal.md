## Core identity
- **Birú** = indigenous people of the Isthmus of Balboa (user-named 2026-05-17)
- **Birú Dale** = the Birú language (from Birú *dale* = speech/tongue)
- Province names **Jala** (*yala* = land/territory/mountain) and **Nurra** (*nurra* = sea) are Volisanian corruptions of Birú Dale words
- Pre-colonial socio-political unit: **sahilato** chiefdom; leader = **sahila**
- Matrilineal society; sahila typically male but chosen by senior women of ruling lineage
- Contact population 500K–1M → <50K within a century (disease + conquest + encomienda)

## Documented Birú Dale vocabulary (for future use)
*yala* (land/territory/mountain), *nurra* (sea), *iwa* (clear), *rakí* (reflection/surface), *warakí* (peaceful water), *kari* (trader/bargainer — "Kari Mario"), *sahila* (village leader), *sahilato* (chiefdom), *tule* (person), *mola* (shirt/clothing), *sappi* (village), *ogob* (coconut), *cayuca* (canoe), *uggub* (water)

## Demographics
- National: ~1,057,000 (3%, 2026) — from Demographics of Balboa
- Concentrated: rural Jala (Altbirú, Bosc Verd, Portell comarcas), Tramuntana highlands (Birú Nord, La Serra de Llevant, El Pic del Nord, Els Cims), Nurra northern interior
- Nurra Birú = historically the coastal Nurra chiefdoms; distinct from highland Birú of Jala/Tramuntana
- Higher fertility than national average; lower life expectancy
- Median income ~$2,100 (Jala, <1/3 national median); lowest educational attainment; highest poverty exposure (Tramuntana 42.6%)
