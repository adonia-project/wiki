# Montserrat Riera i Sola — Internal Notes

## Article location

## Established facts (from existing wiki)
- Born 1948 in Sant Bart
- Moved to Sant Cristòfor in the early 1970s
- Worked as scriptwriter for Cinema Lliure before leaving to make independent films
- First film: Les Dones del Port (1981), documentary, minimal budget, screened at Sant Cristòfor Cultural Centre
- Second film: El Desig (1987), triggered obscenity trial
- Third film: La Llibertat de les Dones (1991), most accomplished feminist film of the era
- Prosecuted under Public Decency Act, acquitted by Constitutional Court (22 September 1988)
- Defence led by Magda Vives i Cardó (BCLU)
- Jordi Molins i Castell and Kwame Rovira testified for defence
- Defence committee became Cultural Defence Coalition
- Not a UAC member during Gilded Age (underground filmmakers excluded)
- UAC retrospective in 2002 (first underground filmmaker honoured)
- UAC Lifetime Achievement Award in 2003
- Leading feminist filmmaker of the Gilded Age
- Associated with Sant Cristòfor Cultural Centre and Cinema Independent
- Films circulated through community centres and informal networks
