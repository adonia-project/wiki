# Fosian Balboan people — Internal Notes

## Article Type
Pan-ethnic category article covering all Fosian-origin subgroups in Balboa. Modeled on the Fosian Baliscans article structure but with more detail (richer source material available).

## Key Decisions
- Population: 5,285,000 (15% of national population) — confirmed by user. Many Fosian Balboans classified as Mixed (especially Sinchew-Black Balboan), so 5.3M only counts those who identify as Fosian.
- Primary regions corrected from "Estret, Migjorn, Novara" to "Estret, Migjorn, Sant Llàtzer" per user instruction. Novara is NOT a concentration point.
- Subgroup ordering: Sinchewese (largest) > Zong > Louyang > Other Fosian > Nou-Dagiti > Nakamian > Fosian Liba — per user guidance.
- All three largest subgroups (Sinchewese, Zong, Louyang) originate from the Jade Peninsula.

## Migration Timeline (Chronological)
1. **Secret Trade escapees (17th-18th c.)** — Dagiti Liba / Fosian Liba; from Balausia Peninsula; Anitist; ~52K today
2. **Sinchew War (1648-1656)** — Baiduist refugees; first Sinchewese presence; small numbers
3. **Sinchewese Revolutionary War (1726-1733)** — Sinchew independence from Zong; economic migrants
4. **Zong Balboa period (1899-1950)** — Major influx:
   - Zong (administrative/commercial elite, 15% of Zong Balboa pop at 1934 peak)
   - Sinchewese (working class, 18% of Zong Balboa pop at peak)
   - Louyang (merchants/professionals, smaller)
   - Nakamian (from Nakamizu, artisans/professionals/workers)
5. **GAW siege (1935-1947)** — Zong evacuated (15%→2%), Sinchewese stayed (18%→14%)
6. **Post-reunification (1960-present)** — Community integrated; cultural protections under Twelve Guarantees
7. **Recent immigrants (1990s-present)** — Nou-Dagiti (~220K) + small professional influx from Zong/Sinchew/Nakamizu
