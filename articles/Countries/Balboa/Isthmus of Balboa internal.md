# Isthmus of Balboa — Internal Notes

## Overview
- Article on the isthmus as a geographic/biogeographic feature, not just a political entity
- Bio-focused per user request: heavy on ecology/biogeography, lighter on political history
- The isthmus only includes Jala, Estret, and Migjorn provinces (NOT Tramuntana or Nurra — those are north of the isthmus proper)
- Two sovereign territories: Balboa (3 provinces) + Palpoa Province (Sinchew)
- International City of Areza: neutral enclave, not subject to either
- Area: ~138,000 km² total (~113,000 Balboan + ~24,000 Palpoa)

## Key Decisions
- **Palpoa Province**: Sinchew's province on the isthmus, created from territory ceded after Second War of the Isthmus (1899). Area ~24,000 km², pop ~4.8M, capital Palpoa (city). Provides Sinchew direct access to canal's southern approaches.
- **Great Adonian Interchange**: Named the biotic exchange event when the isthmus formed (~3-5 Ma), analogous to the Great American Interchange. Lurandian groups generally outcompeted Abyalan groups.
- **Endemism**: Heavy on cloud forest endemism — birds, amphibians, orchids, bromeliads restricted to specific peaks/valleys
- **Palpoa ecology**: Heavily developed/deforested — centuries of plantation agriculture + urban expansion have removed almost all lowland forest; cloud forest zone narrower and more fragmented; several endemic species locally extirpated or confined to tiny remnant patches; few surviving fragments are active conservation concern
- **Migjorn**: Also heavily deforested on the coastal plain; Sant Cristòfor metro expansion

## Geology
- Isthmus formed by closure of ancient seaway ~3-5 Ma (Abyalan plate colliding with Lurandian margin)
- Tectonically active: moderate earthquakes, volcanic eruptions; most recent significant eruption late 19th century
- Yalabirga within isthmus: 600–1,050m (lower than northern peaks outside isthmus proper); Pico Yalabirga is NOT in the isthmus area
- Cloud forest zone: ~700–1,050m (lower than Balboa country article which covers the full range)
- Highland valleys: 400–800m

## Ecosystems (5)
1. Yalabirga cloud forests (700–1,050m) — most biodiverse, centre of endemism, national parks since 1980s
2. Aethiopian lowland tropical forests — most extensive, heavily cleared, best stands in south + Palpoa
3. Kendriyan dry forests — rain shadow, drought-deciduous, thorn-scrub, vulnerable to fire + ranching
4. Mangrove/estuarine — Aethiopian coast, Estret/Migjorn, nursery habitat, storm protection
5. Freshwater — rivers, Lake Alvarado (artificial), highland lakes/wetlands

## Conservation
- Threats: deforestation, water pollution, overfishing, invasive species, urban expansion, climate change (cloud forest zone shifting upward)
- Both Balboa + Sinchew have national parks; bilateral conservation agreement
- AC: Yalabirga cloud forest = World Heritage Site; AC Convention on Biodiversity
- Enforcement uneven; deforestation rate still exceeds reforestation

## Climate
- Same as Balboa article: tropical, wet May–Nov, dry Dec–Apr
- Aethiopian: 26–29°C, 1,500–2,500mm; Kendriyan: 800–1,200mm (rain shadow)
- Highlands: 18–22°C, 2,000–3,500mm; cloud forest >700m persistent mist
- Tropical storms Aug–Oct on Aethiopian coast; ENSO variability

## Political Geography
- Balboa's isthmus portion: Jala + Estret + Migjorn (~113,000 km², ~28.8M)
- Palpoa (Sinchew): ~24,000 km², ~4.8M
- Areza: ~485 km², ~3M
- Total: ~138,000 km², ~36.6M
