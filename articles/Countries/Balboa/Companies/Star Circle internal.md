# Star Circle — Internal Notes

## Overview
- Star Circle is the frequent flyer program of [[Aeroístmus]]
- Currency: Aero Points
- Alliance: Full Adonian Arrow alliance member — earn AND redeem across all member airlines
- Launched: 2008

## Earning

### Base earn rate
- 5 Aero Points per $1 spent on base fare + carrier surcharges (excludes government taxes/fees)
- Currency: $ (international dollar, earth dollar equivalent)
- Applies to all Aeroístmus flights and all Adonian Arrow alliance partner flights

### Elite bonus multipliers
| Tier | Bonus on base points |
|------|---------------------|
| Member | 0% |
| Silver | +25% |
| Gold | +75% |
| Platinum | +100% |
| Diamond | +150% |

Example: Gold member buys $2,000 SCI→Aoyama-Maekawa RT → 5 × 2,000 = 10,000 base + 75% bonus = 17,500 Aero Points

## Tiers

### Qualification
- Points only (no segment count alternative)
- Calendar year qualification (Jan–Dec)
- No soft landing — must requalify from scratch each calendar year

| Tier | Points needed | Bonus | 
|------|--------------|-------|
| Member | 0 | 0% |
| Silver | 25,000 | +25% |
| Gold | 50,000 | +75% |
| Platinum | 75,000 | +100% |
| Diamond | 125,000 | +150% |

### Elite benefits (standard set)
- Silver: Priority check-in, +1 bag, priority boarding
- Gold: + Silver + lounge access, upgrade priority
- Platinum: + Gold + extra baggage, priority security, companion lounge access (1 guest)
- Diamond: + Platinum + guaranteed seat, invitation events

### Companion lounge access
- Platinum and Diamond only — may bring 1 guest into the lounge

### Lifetime status (points-based)
| Lifetime points earned | Lifetime tier |
|------------------------|---------------|
| 500,000 | Gold |
| 1,000,000 | Platinum |
| 2,000,000 | Diamond |

## Award chart

### Zones
1. Domestic (within Balboa)
2. Lurandia + Abyala continents
3. Fosia / Illypnia / Kaftia

### One-way award prices (economy)
| Zone | Economy | Premium Eco | Business | First |
|------|---------|-------------|----------|-------|
| Domestic (Balboa) | 8,000 | 12,000 | 16,000 | 24,000 |
| Lurandia + Abyala | 20,000 | 30,000 | 40,000 | 60,000 |
| Fosia/Illypnia/Kaftia | 40,000 | 60,000 | 80,000 | 120,000 |

- Cabin multipliers: Economy 1x, Premium Economy 1.5x, Business 2x, First 3x
- Round-trip = 2x one-way
- Any seat available as award — no blackout dates, no limited award inventory
- Alliance redemption: can redeem Aero Points on all Adonian Arrow partner flights (including Sanesair)

### Cabin upgrades
- Yes, on eligible fare classes only (higher economy fares, not cheapest)
- Eligible fare classes: full fare economy only (most flexible economy fares, not discounted/restricted)
- Upgrade from economy to premium economy, business, or first depending on zone and fare class

## Point expiry
- No expiry as long as account is active
- Active = at least 1 flight (Aeroístmus or alliance partner) every 24 months
- No family/household pooling — individual accounts only

## Non-air partners

### Banc Balboa co-branded credit card (3 tiers)
| Card tier | Aeroístmus spend | Everyday spend | Elite status |
|-----------|-----------------|----------------|-------------|
| Standard | 3 pts/$1 | 1 pt/$1 | None |
| Gold | 5 pts/$1 | 1 pt/$1 | None |
| Platinum | 10 pts/$1 | 1.5 pts/$1 | Automatic Gold |

- Banc Balboa (SCX: BB) is Balboa's largest bank
- Card earns Aero Points on all purchases; Aeroístmus spend at elevated rates
- Points earned from card spending do NOT count toward tier qualification
- Banc Balboa Platinum card comes with automatic Gold status

### Grup Pastel (hotels)
- 2 Aero Points per $1 spent on hotel stays
- Grup Pastel (SCX: GPT) is Balboa's largest hospitality/tourism group
- 42 properties across the isthmus (luxury, premium, mid-range, budget)
- Also package holidays, tours, cruise excursions, F&B

### IstmeLloguer (car rental)
- 1 Aero Point per $1 spent on car rentals
- Lloguer i Viatges de l'Istme, S.A. (SCX: LVI), trading as IstmeLloguer
- 12 rental locations nationwide, ~4,200 vehicle fleet
- Also operates coach tours

### HRX (retail — transfer partner)
- HRX loyalty points can be transferred to Aero Points at a 1:1 ratio
- HRX, S.A. (SCX: HRX) is Balboa's largest department store and retail company
- Operates department stores, shopping centres, and Botiga Ràpida convenience stores
- Transfer partner (not direct earn) — members accumulate HRX loyalty points through retail purchases and transfer to Star Circle
