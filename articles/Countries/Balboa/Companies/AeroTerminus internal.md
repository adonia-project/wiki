# AeroTerminus — Internal Notes

## Overview
- Regional/charter airline based at Illa Daurada Airport (IDA)
- IATA: B8, ICAO: BTA, Callsign: TERMINUS
- Independent company (not a subsidiary of Aeroístmus, despite dry lease arrangement)
- Founded 1977 — at the end of the 1968–1978 Balboa recession and beginning of the Balboa real-estate bubble (1978–1987)
- Originally charter for wealthy residents: SCI, PNU, IDA, SBT
- Flew Boeing 737-200
- Real-estate bubble burst 1987 → Balboa real-estate asset crisis (1985–1991) → Banc Balboa insolvent mid-1988 → AeroTerminus bankrupt 1988
- Restructured → regional scheduled carrier
- Current: IDA → PNU, SBT (scheduled, 2 ATR 72-600s); IDA → CBL, EAI (charter, 1 Beechcraft 1900)

## Key People
- **Founder**: Francesc Riera (c. 1935–2001) — former naval aviator from Illa Daurada, founded AeroTerminus in 1977, ousted during 1988 bankruptcy
- **Current CEO**: Marta Riera (b. c. 1968) — daughter of founder, joined restructured airline in 1990 as ground ops assistant, worked her way up, became CEO in 2012, led fleet transition from BAe 146 to E175 (c. 2014), then E175 to ATR 72-600 (c. 2024)

## Financial Data (2024)
- **Revenue**: ~B4.5 million (Balboan dollars/international dollars)
- **Employees**: ~30
- **Fleet**: 3 aircraft (2 ATR 72-600 + 1 Beechcraft 1900)
- **Status**: Independent, small profit / break-even operation

## Historical Context (from existing wiki articles)
- **1968–1978 Balboa recession**: Fiscal shock from integrating AC mandate territory, withdrawal of AC subsidies, currency overvaluation. Government liberalised foreign real-estate ownership to attract capital.
- **Balboa real-estate bubble (1978–1987)**: Foreign capital inflows, Banc Balboa absorbent lending, property price inflation in Sant Cristòfor and Portnou. Bubble burst 1987 when Illypnian/Fosian interest rates rose.
- **Balboa real-estate asset crisis (1985–1991)**: Bank failures, Banc Balboa insolvency (mid-1988), government/ACMF bailout. Arrest of Tân Hông-un (1989).
