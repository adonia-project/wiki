# RapidAir (Balboa) — Internal Notes

## Overview
- Low-cost carrier, second-largest airline in Balboa after Aeroístmus
- IATA: RB, ICAO: RAPI, Callsign: RAPID
- Independent company
- Founded 1984 by Jordi Mas i Vidal (b. c. 1945), Portnou shipping/transport entrepreneur
- HQ: Portnou, Estret Province
- Base: Portnou International Airport (PNU)
- Hubs: SCI (Sant Cristòfor), SBT (Sant Bart)

## Key People
- **Founder**: Jordi Mas i Vidal (b. c. 1945) — Portnou shipping/transport entrepreneur, founded RapidAir in 1984 during the real-estate bubble
- **Current CEO**: Núria Bosch (b. c. 1970) — aviation management background, joined 2005, CEO since 2015, led A320 fleet renewal and international expansion

## Financial Data (2024)
- **Revenue**: ~B180 million
- **Employees**: ~450
- **Fleet**: 14 aircraft (8 A320 + 2 A321 + 4 ATR 72-600)
- **Passengers**: ~2.5-3 million/year (estimated)
- **Status**: Independent, profitable

## History Timeline
1. **1984**: Founded by Jordi Mas i Vidal, operates from Ciutat de l'Estació Airport (main SC airport at the time)
2. **1985**: SCI (Sant Martí) opens; old airport redesignated Areza International Airport; RapidAir relocates SC operations to SCI
3. **1987**: Real-estate bubble bursts
4. **1985–1991**: Real-estate asset crisis — wealthy clientele disappears
5. **c. 1990–1992**: Pivots to low-cost carrier model
6. **1992**: PNU opens; RapidAir moves principal operations from SCI to PNU (home city advantage)
7. **1993**: Runway incursion at SCI (737 vs Aeroístmus A310)
8. **2003**: International flights from PNU (Tamsui, Gran Port de Sant Mateu)
9. **2003**: Areza International Airport (old Ciutat de l'Estació) closes
10. **2013**: Major fleet renewal — A320 family replaces 737s
11. **2023**: International expansion — SCI → Tamsui

## Services
- Single-class economy on all aircraft
- Base fare: seat + one personal item only
- Extra-fee services: carry-on (10kg), checked bag (20kg), early boarding, exit row seat, first row seat, standard seat selection
- Buy-on-board: snacks, sandwiches, beverages; hot meals on international + domestic flights >90 min
- No traditional frequent flyer programme; RapidClub subscription ($10/month) = free seat selection + free checked bag

## Accidents and Incidents
1. **3 Sep 1993** — Runway incursion at SCI. RapidAir 737-300 entered Rwy 09/27 without clearance; Aeroístmus A310 aborted take-off at ~120 kts, stopped ~400m short. No injuries. Cause: crew misinterpreted taxi clearance as line-up clearance; ATC phraseology ambiguous. Result: mandatory readback procedures + enhanced taxiway lighting at SCI.
2. **17 Nov 2008** — RapidAir Flight 412, ATR 72-210, runway excursion at PNU. Arriving from Campdària in heavy rain. Hydroplaning + crosswind. Veered off runway onto grass. No injuries among 64 pax + 4 crew. Minor landing gear damage. Result: revised wet-runway procedures, increased min visibility for ATR 72 at airports without runway grooving.
