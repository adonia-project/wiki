# Transabyala AirFreight — Internal Notes

## Overview
- Private independent Balboan cargo airline
- Based at Portnou International Airport (PNU)
- Founded 2005
- Focus: Lurandia/Abyala regional cargo routes
- Only dedicated cargo airline based in Balboa

## Fleet
- 3 × Airbus A321-200(P2F) — converted passenger-to-freighter aircraft
- Total fleet: 3 aircraft (narrowbody freighters)

## Routes
- Base: Portnou (PNU)
- Destinations: Tamsui, Gran Port de Sant Mateu, Sanu-Sasso, Guledga, Nanaimo, Kanakou
- Nanaimo and Kanakou are in Kaneda (not Sinchew)
- All destinations are in Lurandia/Abyala region

## Naming
- "Transabyala" = flying across the Abyala region
- IATA: TA, ICAO: TBA, Callsign: TRANSABYALA

## Ownership
- Private independent company
- Founded by Portnou-based logistics entrepreneurs (2005)

## Operations
- Proximity to Balboa Canal port at Portnou — canal's Shendan terminus
- Carries perishable agricultural products, time-sensitive manufactured goods, high-value items
- Supplements sea freight for cargo requiring faster transit

## Revenue/Employees
- Revenue: ~$28 million (2024)
- Employees: ~80

## Relationship to other airlines
- Aeroístmus Cargo: Aeroístmus's cargo division (1 × 777F for Volisania route + belly cargo)
- Transabyala: separate company, different market focus (regional Lurandia/Abyala vs. long-haul Volisania)
- Both operate from different airports (Aeroístmus Cargo at SCI, Transabyala at PNU)
