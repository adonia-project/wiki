# Sartisanian language — internal notes

## Article info

## Key design decisions
- Sartisanian = Volisanian with Sartan vocabulary mixed in (IRL: Catalan with Spanish words)
- NOT a creole or contact language — both parent languages are closely related Mesogean Romance languages
- Lexical blending only: Volisanian grammar/phonology/syntax, Sartan words substituted for common terms
- Domains of Sartan vocabulary: food, family, religion, rural life
- No official status, no standardisation, no literary tradition — oral/domestic use only
- ~30,000 fluent speakers (fewer than 5% of Sartan Balboans), in decline
- Concentrated in Sant Cristòfor, Sant Bart, and rural Tramuntana
- Guajicutea-originated Sartans (1824) contributed to the bilingual environment, NOT to a separate substrate
- User clarification (2026-09-03): Sartisanian is "just a mix of sartan words with volisanian" — not a deep structural contact language

## IRL mapping
- Sartan (without Arabic) = Spanish
- Volisanian = Catalan
- Sartisanian ≈ Catalan with Spanish vocabulary substitutions (like a Spanish-influenced Catalan register)
