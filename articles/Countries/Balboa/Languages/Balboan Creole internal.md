# Balboan Creole — Internal Notes

## Quick Reference
- Native name: Kreyòl Balboyen
- Volisanian name: Crioll Balboà
- Speakers: ~15.8M (45% L1, ~25% L2)
- Classification: Volisanian-lexifier creole, Kaftian grammatical substrate
- Script: Echian (standardised 2018); Volisanian orthography (historical)
- Regulatory body: Acadèmia del Kreyòl Balboyen (est. 2012)
- Status: Vernacular (no official status); Volisanian is sole official language

## Key Features
- **Vocabulary**: 85-90% Volisanian-derived
- **Grammar**: Fundamentally Kaftian — SVO with serial verbs, no inflectional morphology, preverbal TAM markers, tonal system
- **Tone**: Two-tone system (high/low) — lexical + grammatical function; most fully realised in basilect
- **Substrate languages**: Primarily Ayorin and Dakari

## Vocabulary Sources
| Source | % | Domains |
|--------|---|---------|
| Volisanian | 85-90% | General vocabulary |
| Kaftian (Ayorin/Dakari) | ~5-8% | Spirituality, music, food, kinship |
| Fosian (Zong/Sinchewese) | ~2-3% | Commerce, technology, urban life |
| Birú Dale | <1% | Natural environment terms |

## Grammar Highlights
- **Serial verb constructions**: Li pran liv ale (He take book go) = "He took the book and left"
- **TAM markers**: te (completed), pe (ongoing), va (future), ta (hypothetical)
- **No verb conjugation**: Tense via preverbal particles
- **No noun gender/case**: Plurality via prenominal particle or context
- **Negation**: pa (preverbal, from Volisanian pas)
- **Possession**: Juxtaposition — jan liv (Jan book) = "Jan's book"

## Varieties
- **Basilectal**: Plantation Creole — more Kaftian features, fuller tonal system, more Kaftian vocabulary, less Fosian influence
- **Mesolectal**: Urban Creole (Sant Cristòfor) — closer to Volisanian lexifier, Fosian lexical influence, reduced tonal distinctions
- **Continuum**: Sant Cristòfor speakers operate across basilect–acrolect continuum

## Standardisation Timeline
- Pre-2012: Ad hoc Volisanian orthography (ill-suited to phonology/tone)
- 2012: Acadèmia del Kreyòl Balboyen established
- 2018: Echian-based orthography adopted
- **Controversy**: Echian script affirms Kaftian heritage but severs visible link to Volisanian etymology; Democratic Alliance opposes; both orthographies still in use

## Literature
- **Elena Vargas** (1932–1989): Foremost Creole literary figure; 3 poetry collections (1964-1981)
- **Hái-khiⁿ** journal (1956–1978): Principal venue for Creole literature
- **La Voz del Istmo** (1953–): Creole weekly
- **Istmo son**: Most important vehicle for Creole cultural production
- **Prèmi Vargas**: Annual literary prize (Acadèmia), for works in standardised orthography

## Political Status
- **BPP**: Periodically proposes co-official status
- **Democratic Alliance**: Opposes — argues Volisanian-only needed for commerce + Fosian/Birú integration
- **Socialist Front**: Supports co-official status — current diglossia privileges Volisanian speakers
