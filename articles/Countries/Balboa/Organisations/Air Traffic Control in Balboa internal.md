# Air Traffic Control in Balboa — Internal Notes

## Overview
ATC in Balboa is operated by the Balboa Airports Authority (BAA) through its Air Navigation Services Division. Balboa's compact airspace (isthmus country, ~34B GDP, 23 airports) requires a relatively simple ATC structure compared to large countries.

## ATC Structure

### 1. Balboa Area Control Centre (ACC)
- 1 ACC, located near SCI (busiest airport, central to airspace)
- Handles en-route control for all Balboan airspace
- Collocated with SCI approach control
- Staff: ~45 (controllers + supervisors, 3 shifts)
- Operating cost: ~$18M/year (radar systems, flight data processing, comms, controller salaries)

### 2. Approach Control (6 stations)
- At all 6 international airports
- SCI: dedicated approach control (high volume), ~$8M/year, ~25 staff
- SBT: ~$3M/year, ~12 staff
- PNU: ~$2.5M/year, ~12 staff
- BDC, SLZ, HAI: ~$1.5M/year each, ~12 staff each
- Total approach: ~$18M/year, ~85 staff

### 3. ATC Towers (14 towers)
- All 6 international airports: Tower + Approach
- 8 major domestic airports (>200K pax): Tower only
  - Novara, Vellmar, Campdària, Portblanc, Costabella, Altaneu, Marie Torres, Illes del Guano
- Staffing: 5-12 per tower depending on traffic volume
- Total towers: ~$19.4M/year, ~137 staff

### 4. AFIS Stations (9 stations)
- 9 smaller domestic airports (<200K pax): Aerodrome Flight Information Service
  - Platja d'Aro, Amelio Perez, Santa Caterina, L'Estany, Sant Cristòfor Els Aiguamolls, Foia Gran, Illa Daurada, Sant Jorge, Sant Martí del Nord
- Not full ATC — advisory service only, no active control
- Staff: ~3 per station
- Total AFIS: ~$2.25M/year, ~27 staff

### 5. Navigation Aids
- VOR/DME, ILS at all airports with jet capability
- Maintenance: ~$4.6M/year across 23 airports
