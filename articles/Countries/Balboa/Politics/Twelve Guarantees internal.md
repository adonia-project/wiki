# Twelve Guarantees — Internal Notes

## Quick Reference
- Balboan Creole: Las Doce Garantías
- Enacted: 14 September 1960 (Treaty of Balboa Harbour)
- Entrenched in Constitution of Balboa; cannot be altered without AC consent
- Drafted by AC legal office + Sant Cristòfor Consultative Assembly
- Product of No Return movement pressure

## The Twelve Guarantees (Summary)

| # | Name | Summary |
|---|------|---------|
| 1 | Full civil rights | Vote, hold office, own property — no racial restriction |
| 2 | Anti-discrimination | Employment, housing, commercial — enforceable by independent courts |
| 3 | Non-white representation | Minimum seats in legislature for former mandate territory |
| 4 | Zong/Creole education | Right to Zong-language and Creole-language public schools |
| 5 | Multilingual publication | Zong, Creole, Sinchewese publications without censorship |
| 6 | Reparations | Land grants, scholarships, capital endowments to descendants of enslaved |
| 7 | AC enforcement | AC can challenge Balboan laws before AC Tribunal |
| 8 | Canal oversight | Canal under international oversight (AC Canal Commission) |
| 9 | ICPF | International Cooperative Police Force under AC authority |
| 10 | Areza | International City of Areza — permanently neutral, AC-governed |
| 11 | Right of petition | Former mandate population can petition AC directly |
| 12 | Entrenchment | Balboa cannot alter/repeal Guarantees without AC consent |

## Key Institutions Created
- **Balboan Labour Authority** (1963) — enforces Second Guarantee (anti-discrimination)
- **Balboan Land and Reparations Authority** (1962–1998) — administered Sixth Guarantee (reparations + land reform)
- **Balboan Investment Authority** — screens foreign investment under Guarantees framework
- **AC Canal Commission** — oversees canal under Eighth Guarantee
- **ICPF** — Ninth Guarantee; Balboa mandate terminated 1990s; Areza mandate perpetual
- **AC Garrison Areza** — military base in Areza

## Political Positions
- **BPP**: Supports Guarantees as foundation of post-reunification order; defends entrenchment
- **Democratic Alliance**: Wants renegotiation; phase out AC enforcement, ICPF, entrenchment as Balboa demonstrates compliance
- **Socialist Front**: Defends Guarantees but wants expansion — more reparations, stronger anti-discrimination, extend cooperative model, oppose weakening AC enforcement
- **Balboan Sovereignty Movement**: Small nationalist formation; wants complete abrogation; not electorally significant

## Legal Challenges (AC Tribunal)
1. **1970s**: Balboa tried to restrict anti-discrimination to former mandate territory only → Tribunal ruled Guarantees apply nationally
2. **1980s**: Balboa tried to limit reparations to descendants of persons enslaved within Balboa (excluding those enslaved elsewhere and transported) → Tribunal ruled broad interpretation
3. **1990s**: Coalition challenged ICPF mandate reduction as premature → Tribunal ruled reduction consistent with Guarantees (Ninth Guarantee allows adjustment based on compliance); Areza mandate perpetual

## Key Relationships
- **Treaty of Balboa Harbour** (1960) — enacted the Guarantees; returned Sant Cristòfor to Balboa
- **Treaty of Areza** (1973) — returned canal zone to Balboa; established AC Canal Commission; preserved Areza
- **No Return movement** — political pressure that forced AC to impose Guarantees
- **Balboa Boba** — white planter oligarchy of rump state; the regime the Guarantees were designed to prevent recurring
- **Zong Balboa** — previous administration; Guarantees protected Zong/Creole linguistic heritage from this era
