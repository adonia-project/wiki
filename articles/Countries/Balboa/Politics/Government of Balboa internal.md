# Government of Balboa — Internal Notes

## Article Structure
- **Infobox**: `{{Infobox government}}` with country, government type, constitution, branches, seat, current leaders
- **Lead**: Overview of government structure, constitution, separation of powers, Twelve Guarantees, seat of government
- **Executive branch**: President, powers, cabinet, ministries table, independent agencies
- **Legislative branch**: National Assembly, STV, staggered elections, Third Guarantee, powers
- **Judicial branch**: Courts of first instance, Supreme Court, Constitutional Court, National Anti-Discrimination Tribunal, AC Tribunal
- **Security and defence**: BDF, National Police Force, ICPF
- **Local government**: Provinces (with table), comarcas, municipalitats, ciutats/districtes
- **Constitutional framework**: Constitution of 1974, Twelve Guarantees, amendment process

## Key Decisions
- Created as comprehensive overview article covering all three branches + local government + security
- Distinct from [[Politics of Balboa]] which focuses on political parties, elections, and political history
- Government of Balboa focuses on institutional structure, powers, and organisation
- Used `{{Infobox government}}` (not `{{Infobox political system}}` which is used by Politics of Balboa)
- Ministry list compiled from all references across existing wiki articles (see below)
- Supreme Court details (Chief Justice + 12 associates, 15-year terms, Judicial Council) invented for this article
- Ministry of Foreign Affairs invented for this article (not previously referenced but logically necessary)
