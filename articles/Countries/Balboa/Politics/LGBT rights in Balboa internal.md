# LGBT rights in Balboa — Internal Notes

## Overview
- LGBT rights article for Balboa
- Among the most comprehensive in the Abyalan region
- Same-sex sexual activity legal since 1973 (reunification)
- Civil partnerships since 2004
- Same-sex marriage since 2011 (Marriage Equality Act)
- Joint adoption since 2011
- Self-determination gender recognition since 2017 (Gender Recognition Act)
- Conversion therapy ban since 2020 (Conversion Practices Prohibition Act)
- Office of LGBT Affairs established 2023

## Regional Attitudes
- **Estret (Sant Cristòfor)**: High acceptance, >70% support for same-sex marriage in polls since 2015
- **Migjorn (Sant Llàtzer)**: Good acceptance, visible LGBT community among youth
- **Tramuntana**: Conservative, ~40-50% support, religious opposition prominent
- **Nurra**: Conservative, ~40-50% support, rural isolation compounds challenges
- **Generational**: >80% support under 30, ~45% support over 60

## Key Locations
- **Sòtol neighbourhood, Sant Cristòfor**: Cultural centre of LGBT community, emerged in 1980s-90s from deindustrialised Dàrsena warehouses
- First Pride march (1993), first community health clinic (1996)

## Key Legislation
1. Civil Partnership Act (2004) — registered partnerships, inheritance, next-of-kin, limited parental rights
2. Civil Partnership Amendment (2007) — stepchild adoption
3. Constitutional Amendment on Sexual Orientation Protections (2008) — "Fourteenth Guarantee"; sexual orientation added to Twelve Guarantees
4. Marriage Equality Act (2011) — full marriage rights, joint adoption, parental presumption
5. Gender Recognition Act (2017) — self-determination, non-binary recognition, no medical gatekeeping
6. Constitutional Amendment on Gender Identity Protections (2019) — "Fifteenth Guarantee"; gender identity added to protected categories
7. Conversion Practices Prohibition Act (2020) — criminalises conversion therapy for minors

## Constitutional Amendments (the "Guarantees" expansions)
| Colloquial name | Official name | Year | Coverage |
|----------------|---------------|------|----------|
| Thirteenth Guarantee | Constitutional Amendment on Sex-Based Discrimination | 1986 | Sex-based discrimination protections for women |
| Fourteenth Guarantee | Constitutional Amendment on Sexual Orientation Protections | 2008 | Sexual orientation |
| Fifteenth Guarantee | Constitutional Amendment on Gender Identity Protections | 2019 | Gender identity |

Note: "Thirteenth/Fourteenth/Fifteenth Guarantee" are colloquial terms, not official names. The official names are descriptive.

## Institutions
- **National Anti-Discrimination Tribunal**: Adjudicates discrimination complaints
- **Office of LGBT Affairs** (est. 2023): Within Ministry of Social Welfare, coordinates policy, monitors complaints, supports community orgs

## Cross-references

## Court Cases (invented)
| Case | Year | Issue | Outcome |
|------|------|-------|---------|
| Agramunt v. Balboa Pension Fund | 2006–2007 | Civil partner denied survivor pension | Ruled discriminatory; civil partnerships must carry equivalent weight to marriage; catalyst for Marriage Equality Act 2011 |
| Reyes v. Ministry of Health | 2013–2014 | Trans woman denied gender-affirming healthcare (linked to medicalised legal recognition) | Ruled discriminatory; existing gender recognition framework inconsistent with Twelve Guarantees; basis for Gender Recognition Act 2017 |
| Sòtol Community Coalition v. Tramuntana Provincial Government | 2016–2017 | Provincial funding excluded LGBT orgs; curriculum lacked LGBT content | Partial ruling: funding allocation = indirect discrimination (must revise); curriculum = requires legislative resolution → 2019 Inclusive Education Amendment |

## Additional Legislation (invented)
- Inclusive Education Amendment (2019) — mandates all provinces include age-appropriate content on sexual orientation and gender identity in secondary school curricula; arose from Sòtol Community Coalition case
