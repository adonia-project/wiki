# Lim Hui-jin — Internal Notes

## Key Details
- Born May 11, 1964, Original, Sant Cristòfor, Estret, Balboa
- First Fosian-descended President of Balboa (2024–present)
- Party: BPP (Balboan People's Party), Social Democratic faction
- Ethnicity: Zong Balboan (family descended from Zong Balboans who moved during Zong Balboa era 1899–1950)
- Education: University of Sant Cristòfor, political science major
- Daughter: Maria Lim (married to esports player Julio Nakayama)

## Career Timeline
- ~1982: Enrolled at University of Sant Cristòfor, political science
- 1983–1993: Worked under Ferrer administration (President Roser Muntaner i Ferrer) in Ministry of Education (while at university)
- ~1986: Graduated; continued as administration staff in Ministry of Education
- ~1990s: Transferred to Ministry of Technology
- Early 2000s: Early member of Social Democratic faction (Facció Socialdemòcrata) within BPP
- 2004: Ousted from Ministry of Technology (factional pressure from Centrist wing)
- 2004: Ran for National Assembly, Estret Province — won
- 2004–2024: Served as National Assembly member for Estret
- 2022: Announced presidential campaign
- 2024: Won presidential election; first Fosian-descended president; first BPP president without Assembly majority

## Political Context
- BPP has three factions: Leftist (oldest, smallest), Social Democratic (younger, progressive), Centrist (largest, establishment)
- Lim is in the Social Democratic faction — seen as progressive activist
- Centrist faction had produced most recent presidents; Lim's rise challenges that
- 2024 Assembly election: BPP lost majority for first time (~45–50 seats)
- Lim must negotiate with opposition parties to pass legislation

## Family
- Daughter: Maria Lim — married to Julio Nakayama (esports player, 5x GCWC champion)
- Family are Zong Balboans — descended from Zong who settled in Sant Cristòfor during Zong Balboa period (1899–1950)
- Zong Balboan community declined after Great Adonian War; concentrated in Original's eastern neighbourhoods
