# Balboa Football League System — Internal Notes

## Quick Reference
- Organiser: Federació Balboana de Futbol (FBF), founded 1954 as Balboan Republic league governing body; recognised as sole governing body 1960
- First season: 1961 Apertura (unified league); predecessor leagues: Lliga de l'Istme (1953, mandate period), Lliga de la República Balboana (1951, rump state)
- Format: Abyalan split season (Apertura Feb–Jun, Clausura Aug–Dec)
- Champion system: Apertura champion + Clausura champion → Gran Final → overall champion

## Relegation Rules
- **La Lliga → LFB2**: Bottom 2 auto-relegated; 15th enters playoff vs LFB2 3rd
- **LFB2 → LFB3**: Bottom 2 auto-relegated; 16th enters playoff vs LFB3 runner-up (opposite group)
- **LFB3 → LFB4**: Bottom per group auto-relegated; 9th per group enters playoff
- **LFB4 → Regional**: Bottom per group auto-relegated; 5th per group enters playoff
- Relegation based on aggregate table (both tournaments combined), NOT individual tournament

## Key Rivalries
- **Clàssic de l'Istme**: FC Sant Cristòfor vs Club Esportiu Sant Bart — corridor vs interior, wealth vs working class, cosmopolitan vs Creole
- **Derbi de Sant Cristòfor**: FC Sant Cristòfor vs UE Barri Negre — port vs Black quarter
- **Derbi Fosià**: CE Haimêng vs CE de l'Est — Zong vs Sinchewese community

## Club Identities
- **FC Sant Cristòfor**: Port/commercial class; cross-ethnic support; oldest club (1928)
- **CE Sant Bart**: Interior/working class; Black Balboan identity; son de interior/hip hop culture; most titles (14)
- **UE del Mercat**: Black quarter community club; named after the covered market in Barri Negre; Creole-language cultural expression
- **CE Haimêng**: Fosian-heritage (Zong community); 海門 Hái-mn̂g = "Sea Gate"; neighbourhood around old harbour
- **CE de l'Est**: Fosian-heritage (Sinchew community); named after Barri de l'Est (Eastern Quarter)
- **CD Sant Bart B**: Cooperative farm workers' organisations; working-class

## Historical Periods
1. **Origins (1920s–1940s)**: Community clubs formed by port/canal/plantation workers; GAW (1935–1947) suspends all football; clubs dormant during siege
2. **Divided leagues (1951–1960)**: Lliga de la República Balboana (1951, rump state, closed system); Lliga de l'Istme (1953, AC mandate, Abyalan split-season, multilingual, Fosian coaching)
3. **Unification (1960–1973)**: FBF recognised as sole governing body 1960; unified La Lliga de l'Istme 1961 (8+8 clubs); LFB2 established 1973
4. **Clàssic era (1960s–1980s)**: FC Sant Cristòfor vs CE Sant Bart dominance (20 of 25 titles 1960–1985); Fosian clubs emerge (CE Haimêng 1967, CE de l'Est 1972)
5. **Modern era (1990s–present)**: More competitive; LFB3 1982, LFB4 1991; financial gap persists

## Domestic Cups
- **Copa de l'Istme**: Primary cup, all 4 tiers, single-elimination
- **Copa Balboa**: Secondary cup, LFB2 and below, introduced 1988

## Continental Competition
- **Abyalan Champions' Cup**: Overall La Lliga champion + Gran Final runner-up
- **AFC Cup**: Apertura/Clausura champions (if different) + Copa de l'Istme winner
