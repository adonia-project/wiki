# Club Esportiu Sant Bart — Internal Notes

## Key Facts
- Full name: Club Esportiu Sant Bart, S.A.D.
- Founded: 12 March 1934
- City: Sant Bart, Jala Province, Balboa
- Stadium: Estadi Nacional (42,000 capacity)
- La Lliga titles: 14 (most in Balboa)
- Copa de l'Istme: 6
- Copa Balboa: 2
- Nicknames: Els de la Terra (The Earthmen), The Terracers, El Sant Bart

## Key People

- **Yawa Kuri**: Birú striker; 147 goals; 1964 Apertura hero
- **Kwasi "El Fletxa" Osei**: "The Arrow"; 1964 Apertura; died 1968 (different person from Oriol Cardona the journalist)
- **Carles Busquets**: Midfielder; 5 titles (different person from Miquela Aldana the editor)
- **Amara Diallo**: Former player (1988–1999), current manager (2010s–present) (different person from Djanèt Ousmane the journalist)
- **Nadège Charlot**: Midfielder; 2003 Clausura; 42 goals (different person from Elena Vargas the poet)
- **Miquel Xercavins**: Current sporting director
- **Kwame Asante**: Defender; captain; 3 titles; 350 appearances

## Tactical Identity
- **Estil de les graderies** (Terraces style): Quick, improvisational attacking play
- Originated in the Lliga de les Graderies (Terraces League)
- Developed by Arawak Imotu from 1966
- Modernised by Djanèt Ousmane (2010s) with pressing and positional play
- Contrasts with Fosian-influenced structured style of corridor clubs

## Stadium
- Estadi Nacional: Built 1938 as public works project; largest in Balboa (42,000)
- **Graderies**: Open terraces behind goals; Black working class stood here during segregation
- **Grada de la Terra** (Terrace of the Earth): North goal stand; retains cultural significance after 1998 all-seater conversion

## Ownership
- BPP community trust (Balboan People's Party)
- Reflects club's connection to Black Balboan political movement
