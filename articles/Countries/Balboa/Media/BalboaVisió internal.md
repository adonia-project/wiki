# BalboaVisió — Internal Notes

## Overview
- **Name**: BalboaVisió, S.A. ("Balboa Vision")
- **Type**: Private commercial television network, publicly traded (SCX: BVI)
- **Founded**: 1998 (incorporated 15 June 1998, began broadcasting 1 November 1998)
- **Headquarters**: 14 Avinguda dels Estudis, Costa Nord, Sant Cristòfor, Balboa
- **Target audience**: "Unification generation" — people born in the 1970s who grew up after the 1973 reunification
- **Employees**: 3,200 (2026)
- **Financials (2026, invented)**: $2.4B revenue, $420M operating income, $310M net income, $5.8B assets, $4.5B market cap

## Founders (invented)
- **Jordi Calvet i Cardona** — media entrepreneur, built regional radio network in Estret corridor in 1980s
- **Núria Prat i Areny** — television producer, former Telebadia staffer, brought production expertise
- **Pere Serra i Solà** — financier, early Bolsa de Sant Cristòfor investor, provided initial capital

## Current Leadership (invented)
- **Oriol Calvet i Prat** (Chairman) — son of founder Jordi Calvet i Cardona and Núria Prat i Areny
- **Margarida Riera i Quintana** (CEO)

## Ownership (invented)
- Grup Calvet (Calvet family holding): 18%
- BLEC (Balboan media conglomerate): 10%
- Fons d'Inversió Directa de Balboa (FIDB): 6%
- Public float: ~66%

## Divisions
1. **Entertainment division** — original drama, comedy, reality, entertainment formats; ~15-20 original series/year
2. **NBV (Notícies BalboaVisió)** — news division; ~400 journalists; 24-hour news cycle; bureaus in Sant Cristòfor, Tapuya, Port Soledat; one of the major news operations in Balboa
3. **Streaming and digital division** — operates BalboaVisió+ streaming platform (launched 2021)

## Key Invented Shows
- **Generació Unitat** (1999) — first original drama; friends from different ethnic/linguistic backgrounds in Sant Cristòfor; ratings success; established brand identity

## Context within Balboan TV Landscape
- **Existing broadcasters** (from Sant Cristòfor article):
  - Telebadia (public, Volisanian and Creole) — operated by Serveis de Comunicacions de Balboa (SCB)
  - Canal Nord (commercial, Volisanian)
  - Zong TV Balboa (commercial, Zong)
- BalboaVisió is a **new fourth network**, not a rebranding of any existing broadcaster
- All four are headquartered in Costa Nord

## Regional Offices
- Tapuya (sales office est. 2002, production office)
- Port Soledat, Potocsí (production office est. 2005)

## Areas Served
- Balboa, Potocsí, Tapuya (BTPMEU markets)

## Naming
- "BalboaVisió" = Balboa + Visió ("vision" in Volisanian)
- NBV = Notícies BalboaVisió (news division)

## Timeline
- 1998: Founded (15 June incorporation, 1 November first broadcast)
- 1999: First original drama "Generació Unitat" airs
- 2002: Sales office in Tapuya
- 2003: IPO on Bolsa de Sant Cristòfor; BLEC increases to 10%, FIDB acquires 6%
- 2005: Production office in Port Soledat, Potocsí
- 2021: BalboaVisió+ streaming platform launched
- 2026: 3,200 employees, $2.4B revenue
