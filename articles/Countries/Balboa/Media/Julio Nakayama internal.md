# Julio Nakayama — Internal Notes

## Key Details
- Born January 2, 1996, Sant Bart, Jala, Balboa
- Grew up in Zona 6 neighborhood, Sant Bart
- Handles: "Naka" (current), "Naka245" (original)
- Elo: 3,000
- Nationality: Balboan
- Ethnicity: Full Nakamian descent (father: Nakamian national from Nakamizu; mother: 3rd generation Nakamian Balboan)

## Family
- Parents divorced 2000; Julio coped with video games
- Lived between Aoyama, Nakamizu (father) and Sant Bart, Balboa (mother)
- Married to Maria Lim, daughter of President Lim Hui-jin (current President of Balboa, 2024–present, BPP, first Fosian-descended president)

## Career Timeline
- 2015: Formed Correcamins with Aleix Riera + 3 others (Nil Masdeu "Volt", Ryo Sato "Frost", Carles Pons "Echo")
  - Named after a popular Balboan video game from the 2000s
  - Players met online
  - Won inaugural GCWC 2015
- 2016: Won GCWC
- 2018: Won GCWC
- 2020: Won GCWC
- 2022: Recruited with Aleix Riera to Força (superteam backed by Abyala Telecom + BalboaVisió)
- 2023: Won GCWC with Força (5th career title)
- 2024: Conflict with Aleix over playstyle (Julio = team-based, Aleix = carry-oriented); left Força; founded Banc Balboa Istmo Jocs
  - BGCC Summer 2024: 4th/QF (first competitive appearance)
- 2025: BGCC Winter runners-up; BGCC Summer winners; GCWC semifinals
- 2026: BGCC Winter 3rd/SF; BGCC Summer winners (back-to-back); qualified for GCWC 2026

## Total Achievements
- 5× GCWC champion (2015, 2016, 2018, 2020 with Correcamins; 2023 with Força)
- 3× BGCC champion (Winter 2023, Summer 2023 with Força; Summer 2025 with BBIJ)
  - Note: Winter 2024 with Força also counts — need to verify if he was still on roster
- 2× BGCC Summer champion (2025, 2026 with Banc Balboa Istmo Jocs)
