# Banc Balboa Istmo Jocs — Internal Notes

## Overview
- Founded 2024 by Julio Nakayama (former Força member)
- Sponsored by Banc Balboa (existing article: `articles/Countries/Balboa/Economy/Banc Balboa.mediawiki`)
- Operator: Istmo Esports S.A.
- Based in Balboa
- Dark horse reputation
- Won BGCC Summer 2025 and Summer 2026 (back-to-back Summer titles)
- Primary domestic rivals to Força
