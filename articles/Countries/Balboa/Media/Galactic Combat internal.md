# Galactic Combat — Internal Notes

## Overview
Galactic Combat (Volisanian: Combat Galàctic) is a multiplayer fantasy-space themed MOBA developed and published by Jocs Importants, based in Dàrsena, Sant Cristòfor, Balboa. Launched 14 June 2011.

## Key Facts (from user)
- Developer: Jocs Importants (Balboan video game company, based in Darsena, Sant Cristòfor, Balboa)
- First published title of Jocs Importants
- Developers: Job Tan and Mikael Vidal i Torres
- Genre: Multiplayer fantasy-space themed MOBA
- Main mode: Two teams of five players controlling unique "operators" with unique skills and powers
- Teams are in charge of their space colony on two ends of a territory
- Objective: Push towards the opposing colony to take it over
- ~70 million monthly active players
- ~20 million daily players
- Peaked in 2021 with 6 million concurrent players
- 4 different regions where players are ranked on an ELO system
- Top players invited to an international tournament (started 2015)
- Has received: a movie, a trading card game, a spin-off app
- Known as one of the most influential games of the 2010s
- Still one of the most popular games to this day
