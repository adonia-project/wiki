# Aleix Riera — Internal Notes

## Key Details
- Born April 18, 1999, Varakí, Estret, Balboa
- Handle: "Tempesta" (Volisanian for "storm")
- Elo: 3,420 (highest-rated GC player)
- Nationality: Balboan
- Parents: Administrative staff for the Adonian Community
- Education: Attended Varakí Mario Vazques High School; dropped out in 2016 to pursue full-time esports
- Net worth: Estimated $5 million (tournament winnings, sponsorships, streaming)
- Listed among most influential people in Balboan sports

## Career Timeline
- 2015: Co-founded Correcamins with Julio Nakayama + 3 others
  - Won inaugural GCWC 2015
- 2016: Won GCWC
- 2018: Won GCWC
- 2020: Won GCWC
- 2022: Recruited with Julio to Força
- 2023: Won GCWC with Força (5th career title)
- 2024: Won GCWC with Força (6th); Julio left Força mid-2024
- 2025: Won GCWC with Força (7th); started playing ADH; won ADH Grand Championship
- 2026: Retired from GC; full-time ADH player/streamer; ADH Grand Championship runner-up

## Total Achievements
- 7× GCWC champion (2015, 2016, 2018, 2020 with Correcamins; 2023, 2024, 2025 with Força)
  - Record for most GCWC titles by an individual player
- 1× ADH Grand Championship champion (2025)
- ADH Grand Championship runner-up (2026)
- BGCC titles: Winter 2023, Summer 2023, Winter 2024, Summer 2024, Winter 2025 (with Força)

## Rivalry with Julio Nakayama
- Co-founded Correcamins together in 2015
- Won 4 GCWC titles together (2015, 2016, 2018, 2020)
- Both recruited to Força in 2022
- Won 2023 GCWC together
- Conflict over playstyle: Aleix = carry-oriented, Julio = team-based
- Julio left Força in 2024; Aleix stayed and continued winning
- Aleix retired in 2026; Julio still active

## ADH (Aterratge d'Herois)
- Multiplayer FPS survival game
- Volisanian name: "Aterratge d'Herois" = "Landing of Heroes"
- Aleix started playing competitively in 2025
- Won ADH Grand Championship 2025 (while still playing GC)
- Full-time ADH player and streamer from 2026
- Runner-up in 2026 ADH Grand Championship
