# La Voz del Istmo — Internal Notes

## Quick Reference
- Full name: La Voz del Istmo ("The Voice of the Isthmus")
- Creole: Vwa Istmo
- Founded: 12 April 1953 (first issue 19 April 1953)
- Type: Weekly newspaper, broadsheet
- Language: Balboan Creole (primary), Volisanian (secondary)
- Owner: La Voz del Istmo Cooperative (worker-owned since founding)
- HQ: Sant Cristòfor, Estret, Balboa
- Circulation: 85,000 print / 320,000 digital (2026)
- Political alignment: Centre-left; Black Balboan community advocacy
- Status: Oldest continuously published Creole-language periodical in Balboa

## Founding Collective (11 members, 3 named)
- **Oriol Cardona**: Cultural correspondent (1953–1989); coined "istmo son" 1958
- **Miquela Aldana**: First editor-in-chief (1953–1968); Creole poet/essayist; "The Language of the Crossing" essays
- **Djanèt Ousmane**: Labour correspondent (1953–1972); Dakari-descended; "the second men stepping forward" 1955

## Key Phrases Coined
- **"Nou la, nou rest la"** ("We are here, we are staying") — first issue headline, 19 April 1953; became No Return movement rallying cry
- **"The second men stepping forward"** — 1955 editorial; described Black Balboan foremen taking over abandoned Zong enterprises; entered political lexicon
- **"A wall of concrete that could be anywhere in Adonia"** — 1958; described public housing estates

## Historical Arc
1. **1953–1960 (Mandate)**: Founded; No Return movement coalition member; independence advocacy; cultural coverage of Second Star; istmo son named here (1958)
2. **1960–1970s (Early reunification)**: Cautious relationship with BPP; supported land reform/reparations; pressed for Guarantees enforcement in interior; criticised BPP on Creole status and cooperatives
3. **1970s–1980s (Decline)**: Circulation fell as media diversified; Volisanian press dominated; Hái-khiⁿ competition; radio/TV reduced reach
4. **2000s–present (Revival)**: Digital expansion; investigative journalism focus; cooperative farm reform, Guarantees enforcement, Birú community coverage; adopted Echian-script orthography 2018

## Editorial Positions
- Supports: Twelve Guarantees, anti-discrimination enforcement, Creole co-official status, cooperative farm reform
- Opposes: Democratic Alliance Guarantees renegotiation, Balboan Sovereignty Movement abrogation
- BPP relationship: Endorses in every election but regularly criticises (cooperatives, Creole rights, patronage corruption)
