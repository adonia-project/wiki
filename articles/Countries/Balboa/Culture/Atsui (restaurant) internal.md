# Atsui — Internal Notes

## Key Facts
- **Name**: Atsui (熱い, "hot") — Sanese restaurant in Sant Cristòfor, Balboa
- **Founded**: 12 March 2010
- **Owners**: Franz Méliz (b. 1977, front-of-house) and Martin Méliz (b. 1980, head chef)
- **Format**: Omakase only, ~15 courses, 14 seats, single seating per evening
- **Price**: High-end (specific amount TBD)
- **Ratings**: Nº1 Abyala's 50 Best (2015), Nº24 World's 50 Best (2023)

## Brothers' Background
- Born in Okaiken to Balboan parents (father: Sant Cristòfor-born line cook, mother: Sant Bart pastry chef)
- Parents ran a Volisanian-Balboan eatery in Jingō Minato expatriate quarter (1980s–90s)
- Grew up between Sanese kitchen culture and Balboan home cooking
- Martin: informal apprenticeship at washoku counter age 15 → Institut Culinari de Volisania → returned to Okaiken for apprenticeship at mārā gyūbara nabe specialist in Jingō Minato
- Franz: front-of-house manager at high-profile Sanese restaurants in Okaiken capital
- Relocated to Sant Cristòfor 2009

## Cuisine Philosophy
- "Cooking Sanese in Balboan" — NOT fusion in the sense of merging two traditions
- Techniques, plating, meal structure = Sanese
- Ingredients = predominantly local (isthmian seafood, tropical produce, Balboan chilies)
- Rice, soy sauce, miso, koregusu imported from Okaiken (non-negotiable)
- Dashi: blend of imported Okaiken kelp + local isthmian seaweed

## Signature Dish
- **Aka-aburi sashimi** (赤炙り刺身, "red-seared sashimi"): Aethiopian bigeye tuna, Balboan chili–citrus marinade, blowtorch-seared at counter, unseasoned rice, koregusu-infused soy. Adaptation of Okaiken aburi kara-zushi.

## Other Recurring Courses
- Agedashi mapo with Balboan coastal shrimp + local broad-bean paste
- Shima asari no ōsuimono with Shendan-side clams + isthmian kelp
- Seasonal fruit course: Balboan tropical fruits (mango, passionfruit, guava) in Sanese confectionery style

## Dining Room
- 14-seat L-shaped hinoki counter
- Traditional Sanese counter aesthetic
- Single seating per evening
- Reservations required, books months in advance
