## Procedural history (revised 2026-08-27)

The case proceeded through four courts:

1. **Sant Cristòfor High Court** — dismissed charges before trial (early 1988). Court found PDA's definition didn't provide sufficient grounds to proceed; no prima facie case established.
2. **Portblanc High Court** (Tramuntana Province) — case re-filed here because one of the actors in *El Desig* was a Portblanc resident, establishing jurisdiction. Court conducted a trial and ruled unanimously that the film was obscene (March 1988). Defence appealed to Supreme Court.
3. **Supreme Court of Balboa** (Tribunal Suprem, Sant Bart) — appeal review of the Portblanc ruling (April–July 1988). This is where the media attention was concentrated. During the proceedings, the justices recognised a constitutional question (PDA vs. Twelve Guarantees) and deferred it to the Constitutional Court (July 1988).
4. **Constitutional Court of Balboa** (Tribunal Constitucional, Sant Cristòfor) — conducted its own hearing (August 1988). Ruled PDA unconstitutionally vague under the Twelve Guarantees, acquitted Riera i Sola (22 September 1988). Ruling was final. Not appealed.

### Key procedural details
- The re-filing at Portblanc was possible because one of the actors was a Portblanc resident
- Portblanc is the capital of Tramuntana Province (pop. 680,000), on the Shendan coast
- Tramuntana was one of the provinces that had requested the PDA in 1976
- The Supreme Court sat in Sant Bart; the Constitutional Court sat in Sant Cristòfor
- The AC amicus brief was filed with the Constitutional Court
- The government did not appeal the Constitutional Court's ruling

## Key facts from existing wiki

- Film: *El Desig* ("The Desire"), 1987, directed by Montserrat Riera i Sola
- Case: January–September 1988, acquittal by Constitutional Court
- Public Decency Act: 1976, enacted under Torrent i Alvarado, aimed at conservative interior
- President during case: Roser Muntaner i Ferrer (1983–1993, BPP centre-right)
- Defence: led by BCLU, argued Twelve Guarantees protected artistic expression
- Prosecution: argued film gratuitous, violated PDA
- AC human rights directorate filed amicus brief with Constitutional Court
- Government did not appeal Constitutional Court ruling
- PDA amended 1990 to exempt artistic works
- Film preserved at Arxiu Nacional de Cinema de Balboa
- UAC did not take official position; Jordi Molins i Castell publicly supported defence
- Defence committee became nucleus of broader cultural coalition
- *Els Amagats* (1989) produced in aftermath

## Judicial system reference

- Courts of first instance: organised by province (e.g., Sant Cristòfor High Court, Portblanc High Court, Jala Provincial Court)
- Supreme Court of Balboa (Tribunal Suprem): court of final appeal for non-constitutional matters, seated in Sant Bart
- Constitutional Court of Balboa (Tribunal Constitucional): exclusive jurisdiction over constitutional questions and Twelve Guarantees, seated in Sant Cristòfor
- National Anti-Discrimination Tribunal: for discrimination cases
- AC Tribunal (Areza): final authority on Twelve Guarantees matters (appellate body above Constitutional Court)
- The Constitutional Court's ruling could theoretically have been appealed to the AC Tribunal, but the government chose not to appeal

## Media landscape (1988)

### Sant Cristòfor
- *El Faro del Istmo* — newspaper of record, daily, Volisanian
- *La Veu de la Badia* — daily, centre-left, Volisanian
- *La Voz del Istmo* — Creole weekly, Black Balboan community voice
- Telebadia — public TV broadcaster
- Canal Nord — commercial TV, Volisanian

### Interior
- Interior provincial press generally supported prosecution
- Portblanc High Court's obscenity ruling welcomed by Tramuntana press
- Tramuntana Provincial Assembly passed resolution supporting prosecution (May 1988)
- Nurra Provincial Assembly passed similar resolution (June 1988)
- Jala Provincial Assembly debated but did not pass resolution

### Church
- Balboan Catholic Church / Archdiocese of Sant Bart issued statement supporting prosecution (April 1988)
- Archdiocese representative testified for prosecution at Portblanc trial
