# Istmo Son — Internal Notes

## Quick Reference
- Volisanian: Son de l'istme
- Creole: Son Istmo
- Origin: 1950s, Sant Cristòfor, Barri Negre neighbourhood
- Peak: 1960s–1980s
- Named by: Oriol Cardona (1958, La Voz del Istmo article)
- Key instruments: marímbula (bass), claves, congas, bongos, acoustic guitar, trumpet, trombone
- Vocal style: call-and-response (cantor + cor), Creole-language lyrics

## Three Rhythmic Layers
1. **Ground layer**: Kaftian — congas + bongos, polyrhythmic, 2/4 or 4/4 with 6/8 cross-rhythm
2. **Melodic layer**: Volisanian — guitar (màgia arpeggiated figure), brass (punctuation/interludes)
3. **Bass layer**: Kaftian — marímbula (lamellophone), deep resonant bass, simple repetitive line

## Subgenres
| Style | Period | Features |
|-------|--------|----------|
| Son antiguo | 1950s– | Small acoustic ensemble, Creole lyrics, courtyard format |
| Son moderno | Late 1960s– | Brass sections, electric instruments, Fosian jazz influences |
| Son de interior | 1970s– | Slower tempos, sombre themes, no brass, Sant Bart/interior |
| Istmo son electrónico | Mid-1990s– | Electronic production, sampling, digital rhythms + marímbula |
| Son-fusió | Late 1990s– | Fosian jazz harmonies + improvisation |

## Notable Artists (all invented)
- **Barri Negre Trio** (1953–1962): Earliest documented ensemble
- **Dionis Closa** (1955–1988): First commercial recordings 1963; defined small-ensemble format
- **Margarida Fàbrega** (1966–2003): Introduced brass/electric; first international tour 1974 (Zong Commonwealth)
- **Núria Balaguer** (1968–1995): Premier vocalist; Creole lyricism + political commentary
- **Istmo All-Stars** (1971–present): Large ensemble; national radio; bridged antiguo/moderno
- **Sant Bart Collective** (1989–present): Son de interior → hip hop bridge; first Creole hip hop recordings 1991
- **Electra Istme** (1996–present): Electronic istmo son pioneer
- **Quartet Fusió** (1999–present): Son-fusió, Fosian quarter of Sant Cristòfor

## Key Events
- 1953–1955: First courtyard trobades in Barri Negre
- 1958: Llorens article names the genre
- 1963: First commercial recordings (Dionis Closa, Istmo Records)
- 1972: First Festival del Son Istme (annual, Sant Cristòfor)
- 1974: Fàbrega's Zong Commonwealth tour (first international exposure)
- 1980s: Istmo All-Stars Illypnia/Kaftia tours
- 1991: Sant Bart Collective first hip hop recordings

## Key Institutions
- **Istmo Records**: First label to record istmo son (1963)
- **Balboan Cultural Institute**: BPP government body; funded performance/recording
- **Festival del Son Istme**: Annual since 1972, Sant Cristòfor; national musical event
- **Barri Negre**: Historic Black quarter of Sant Cristòfor; birthplace of the genre
