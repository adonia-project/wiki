# Magpalibog — Internal Notes

## Key Facts
- **Name**: Magpalibog (Dagit: "wonder")
- **Founded**: 2013
- **Owner/Chef**: Danilo Kabigting (b. ~1978), Dagit-Balboan from Sant Llàtzer, Migjorn
- **Format**: Tasting menu, ~12 courses, 24 seats, single seating
- **Cuisine**: Elevated Dagit heritage — no dilution, full intensity
- **Rating**: Nº18 Abyala's 50 Best (2023), first entered at Nº34 (2018)
- **Address**: 32nd Floor, International Finance Building, Hotel Senador, 2 Embarcador CR, Sant Cristòfor, Balboa
- **Only Dagit-identified restaurant on the Abyala list**

## Chef Background
- Born c. 1963, Sant Llàtzer, Migjorn
- Family descended from escaped Dagit slaves from Sinchew plantations
- Dagit household, grandmother was plantation cook in Migjorn
- Started in fine dining at a young age, as a teenager in Sant Cristòfor
- Part of first generation of domestic fine-dining operators; before 1991, fine dining was dominated by restaurateurs from abroad
- Widely regarded as one of the founders of domestic fine-dining culture in Balboa
- First restaurant: Hái-khiⁿ (Zong fine dining in Sant Cristòfor), opened 1991, closed 2004
- Second restaurant: L'Alzina (Volisanian restaurant in Sant Cristòfor), opened 2005, still open
- Both were competent but not distinctive — "cooking other people's cuisines"
- Never staged abroad — all experience domestic
- Magpalibog (2013) = first time cooking Dagit food professionally

## Cuisine Philosophy
- Dagit heritage at full intensity — no dilution for Volisanian-trained palate
- Key Dagit elements: vinegar/souring agents, coconut milk, fermented shrimp paste (balaw-balaw), banana-leaf wrapping, open flame grilling/smoking
- Tasting menu progression: cleanest preparations → deeper fermented → richest coconut-milk braises
- Pacing and plating = fine dining; flavors = not

## Signature Dishes
- **Kinilaw**: Raw fish cured in citrus + chili, banana-leaf ash powder
- **Laing**: Taro leaves in coconut milk + fermented shrimp paste + chilies, concentrated on banana-leaf square
- **Sinigang**: Shellfish in tamarind-sour broth, poured at table
- **Final savory**: Braised pork belly in coconut milk + black pepper, rice steamed in banana leaves with coconut cream

## Dining Room
- 24 seats: individual tables + 8-seat counter facing open kitchen
- Dark wood, stone, minimal decoration
- Floor-to-ceiling windows, Shendan harbour view
- Open charcoal grill visible from counter
