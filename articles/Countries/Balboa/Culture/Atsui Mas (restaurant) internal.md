# Atsui Mas — Internal Notes

## Key Facts
- **Name**: Atsui Mas (もっと熱い, "hotter") — bilingual pun: Sanese "atsui" (hot) + Volisanian "más" (more)
- **Founded**: 2017
- **Owners**: Franz Méliz and Martin Méliz (same as Atsui)
- **Format**: Counter-service rice bowls, breakfast + lunch only, no reservations
- **Price**: 4–8 ID per bowl (budget)
- **Address**: 393 Carrer del Canal, Sant Cristòfor, Balboa
- **Neighborhood**: Canal district (hip/creative dining area)

## Cuisine
- **Breakfast** (until ~10:30): Hybrid Balboan-Sanese — scrambled eggs with chili oil, grilled plantain + salted fish with miso, akadon (braised pork + fried egg + koregusu soy over rice)
- **Lunch**: Sanese rice bowls — kararōhan (spicy braised pork rice), mapodon (shrimp agedashi mapo bowl), grilled fish bowl with chili salt cure + pickled vegetables
- **Sides**: Miso soup, pickled papaya, seasonal vegetables
- Rice, soy, miso, koregusu imported from Okaiken; all else local

## Kitchen
- Martin Méliz designs menu, deputy head chef runs day-to-day
- Rapid turnover — most customers in and out in ~15 minutes
- Closes after lunch, no dinner service

## Queues
- 30–45 min weekdays, 1hr+ weekends
- "The line that feeds the neighborhood" (local media)
- Queue is a fixture of Carrer del Canal street life
