## Key Framework
- **4 staple carbs**: Plantains, cassava/root veg, rice, maize
- **Proteins**: Seafood (both coasts), poultry, pork (culturally most significant), limited beef in Jala highlands
- **Chili tradition**: Indigenous Birú chilies (small, intense) + Zongese/Sinchewese imported varieties = dual chili culture
- **Volisanian base**: Full pantry adopted — olive oil, garlic, onions, wine, cured meats, cheese, pork
- **Climate**: Fully tropical, but Jala highlands are cooler (16-22°C), supporting dairy, coffee, temperate veg

## Historical Layers (chronological)
1. **Indigenous Birú** (pre-colonial): Maize, cassava, cacao, fish, game, nixtamalisation, earth ovens, indigenous chilies
2. **Volisanian colonial** (1530s–1854): Full Volisanian pantry imposed, plantation economy, class division in food
3. **Kaftian enslaved layer** (colonial period): Rice cultivation, one-pot stews, okra, leafy greens, deep frying, central starch + side dishes
4. **Fosian layer** (Zong Balboa 1899–1950): Zongese (Sant Cristòfor/canal) + Sinchewese (Migjorn agricultural workers) + Dagit/North Fosian enslaved/indentured (Migjorn plantations)
5. **Post-reunification globalisation** (1973+): International fine dining, Burawi influence in Jala, cosmopolitan Sant Cristòfor

## Regional Summary
| Region | Primary influences | Key characteristics | Staples |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|---------|
| Sant Cristòfor/Estret | Kaftian + Volisanian + Zongese, cosmopolitan | Refined, multi-synthesis, fine dining + street food | Rice, plantains, seafood |
| Migjorn | Sinchewese (working-class Zongese) + Dagit/North Fosian | Spicier, heavier, fermented shrimp paste, one-pot rice dishes | Rice, pork, chilies |
| Jala | Volisanian colonial + Burawi/Volisanian upper-class | Most Volisanian, dairy, cheese, grilled meats, highland produce | Maize, dairy, pork, beef |
| Nurra/Tramuntana | Indigenous + Kaftian + working-class white | Maize/cassava-based, indigenous chilies, simple one-pot, Birú highland traditions | Maize, cassava, seafood |
