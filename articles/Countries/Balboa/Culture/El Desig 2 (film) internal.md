# El Desig 2 (film) — Internal Notes

## Article location
`articles/Countries/Balboa/Culture/El Desig 2 (film).mediawiki`

## User-specified details
- Dark comedy (not a courtroom dramatisation of the trial)
- Very subversive and strange movie
- Got critical acclaim
- Won the Sant Cristòfor Film Festival Best Movie award (2010) → used Costa Nord Film Festival (established in wiki as the oldest/most prestigious in Balboa, in Sant Cristòfor)
- Won the Castejón Film Festival award the following year (2011)
- Plot: fictionalised private investigator from the initial El Desig trial (when it was dismissed), hired by a private investigative unit funded by anonymous conservative groups, never watched the film, tries to find connection between film and Portblanc, breaks into Montserrat's home (real-life burglary reimagined), gets into gay romance, discovers sister is one of the actresses, never knew because never watched the film or asked sister
- The conservative group in the film is called the "Not Catholic Church" — a satirical name, widely theorised to be a front for the Balboan Catholic Church
- IRL: there was a subsequent investigation for a few weeks by a private investigative unit funded by anonymous conservative groups after the initial trial was dismissed
- The conclusion of the movie is the trial being re-filed in Portblanc
- Limited commercial success due to its strangeness; became a cult classic
- Box office: ~$247,000 (estimated); budget: ~$72,000 (estimated)

### Production details (invented)
- Runtime: ~98 minutes
- Shot on digital video (2009, over 8 weeks)
- Budget: modest (Arts Council grant + independent production company)
- Larger budget than earlier films but still modest by mainstream standards
- First Riera i Sola film exhibited in commercial cinemas
- Break-in sequence filmed in Riera i Sola's actual apartment

### Real-life break-in
- In the late 1980s, Riera i Sola's apartment was burgled
- Most likely ordinary theft, NOT state-sanctioned or prosecutor-related
- Rumoured at the time to be connected to the trial
- Riera i Sola reimagined it in the film as the prosecutor's break-in
- She described this as taking ownership of a rumour that had circulated for two decades

### Key themes
- Subversive structure: a "sequel" that is not a sequel; a courtroom drama without a courtroom; a comedy about a trial that never depicts the trial
- The gap between the film as it exists and the film as the state imagines it
- Institutional obsession vs personal knowledge (the answer was in his own family)
- Sexual hypocrisy (decency prosecutor in a gay romance)
- The meaninglessness of bureaucratic obsession
- First Balboan film to win top award at both Costa Nord and Castejón festivals
