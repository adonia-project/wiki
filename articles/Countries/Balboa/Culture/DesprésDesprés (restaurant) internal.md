# DesprésDesprés — Internal Notes

## Key Facts
- **Name**: DesprésDesprés (Catalan "after after") — doubling of "després" (after)
- **Founded**: 2016
- **Owners**: Adelaida Paredes (mother, b. ~1960) and Yolanda Paredes (daughter, b. ~1988)
- **Format**: Family-style tasting menu, groups of 4+ only, "mix" reservation for smaller groups
- **Cuisine**: "Zong restraint, Balboan abundance" — haute Zong minimalism + Black Balboan maximalism
- **Ratings**: Nº4 Abyala's 50 Best (2020), Nº78 World's 50 Best (2024)
- **Address**: 843 Plaça Marcos Aldana, Sant Cristòfor, Balboa
- **Neighborhood**: Historically Black Balboan neighbourhood of Sant Cristòfor
- **Non-alcoholic pairing**: Included in cost, house-made drinks (herbal infusions, fruit shrubs, spiced teas, fermented beverages)

## Owners' Background
- **Adelaida**: 30+ years as line cook in Sant Bart institutional cafeterias (government, hospital, school). Known for personal Black Balboan cooking among colleagues. Never cooked professionally outside cafeteria system. Does not drink alcohol.
- **Yolanda**: Grew up in Adelaida's kitchen → Institut Culinari de Volisania → staged at Volisanian restaurants → returned to Balboa mid-20s. Exposed to Zong culinary tradition in Sant Cristòfor fine dining scene.

## Cuisine Philosophy
- Zong technique + Black Balboan ingredients/flavors
- Served family-style, designed to be shared, not individually plated
- "Zong restraint, Balboan abundance"
- Zong: precision, ingredient integrity, disciplined seasoning, each element distinct
- Black Balboan: layered seasoning, multiple textures, generous portions, overwhelm before satisfy
- Representative course: whole braised chicken (Zong broth precision) + side dishes (chili-oil slaw, cassava + fermented bean paste, citrus/numbing pepper greens, coconut-soy rice)

## Group-Only Policy
- Reservations for 4+ only
- "Mix" reservation (reserva mixta): smaller groups/individuals seated together at communal table, same meal, same cost
- No individual plating offered
- Mix table cited as most memorable way to experience the meal
