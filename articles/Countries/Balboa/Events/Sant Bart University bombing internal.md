# Sant Bart University bombing — Internal Notes

## Key facts
- Date: 12 February 1975
- Time: ~09:15 (during morning lectures)
- Location: Faculty of Law building, Universitat de Sant Bart, Sant Bart, Jala Province
- Weapon: ANFO car bomb (stolen vehicle)
- Deaths: 42 (38 immediate, 4 subsequent)
- Injuries: 78
- Perpetrator: Primera Ordre de Balboa (POB)
- Deadliest terrorist attack in Balboan history

## Perpetrators
- **Joan Ballester i Font** — POB operative, arrested March/April 1975, convicted 1976, life imprisonment
- **Rafel Soler i Mas** — POB operative, arrested March/April 1975, convicted 1976, life imprisonment
- **Pere Vidal i Renom** — POB operative, arrested March/April 1975, convicted 1976, life imprisonment
- Cell of three operatives; broader POB network provided explosives expertise and logistics
- Some network members linked to Murado militia from the Siege of Murado Ranch

## Target
- Universitat de Sant Bart — principal higher education institution in Balboan interior
- Founded 1924 during rump state period as colonial-style white elite institution
- Opened to Black and mixed students under Twelve Guarantees in early 1960s
- By 1975: predominantly Black and mixed student body
- Faculty of Law: largest faculty, symbol of post-Guarantees order, trained lawyers/civil servants for reunified state
- Targeted because it represented integration the POB was fighting to reverse

## Motive
- Retaliation for Siege of Murado Ranch (March 1974)
- Opposition to Twelve Guarantees land redistribution
- Warning against further integration of interior provinces
- POB ideology: racial hierarchy of rump state, Guarantees as unconstitutional infringement

## Investigation
- Conducted by Balboan constabulary with ICPF assistance
- Operatives identified within weeks: witness accounts, vehicle registration, fertiliser tracing
- Arrests in March/April 1975 in Sant Bart and Tramuntana
- Convictions 1976, life imprisonment
- Broader POB network also identified

## Aftermath
- National address by President Alvarado within hours
- BPP called for national unity, demonstrations in Sant Cristòfor
- AC condemned attack, affirmed support for Guarantees
- Cross-racial solidarity: white community leaders in Sant Bart condemned bombing
- Directly triggered La Guàrdia Campaign (1975–1976)
- Led to controversial Biru special arms licenses → 1977 Aeroístmus hijacking

## Legacy
- Faculty of Law building rebuilt, renamed Edifici 42 (Building 42)
- Memorial on campus with names of 42 victims, dedicated 12 February 1976
- Annual commemoration on 12 February
- Political tension: BPP uses anniversary to affirm Guarantees; FSB uses it to criticise BPP liberalisation

## Key dates
- March 1974 — Siege of Murado Ranch (provocation)
- 12 February 1975 — Bombing
- March/April 1975 — Arrests of POB operatives
- 1975–1976 — La Guàrdia Campaign
- 1976 — Convictions, memorial dedicated
- 1977 — Aeroístmus hijacking (consequence of Biru arms programme)
