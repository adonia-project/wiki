# Bank of Balboa — Internal Notes

## Key Facts
- **Official name**: Bank of Balboa (Volisanian: Banc de Balboa)
- **Type**: Central bank
- **Established**: 1968 (during financial system integration post-reunification)
- **Governor**: Tan Vilanova i Ballester (mixed Zong-Volisanian heritage)
- **Headquarters**: 1400 Avinguda Llibertat, El Centre, Original, Sant Cristòfor
- **Coordinates**: (-94.389, 0.635) from POI shapefile
- **Currency**: International Dollar (ID) — shared AC currency
- **Governor appointed by**: President of Balboa
- **Operational independence**: Constitutionally protected

## Monetary Policy Role
- AC participant — implements AC-wide monetary decisions domestically
- Does NOT independently set interest rates or issue currency
- Conducts open market operations for domestic liquidity management
- Holds government bonds for monetary policy purposes
- Maintains settlement and clearing systems connecting to AC monetary infrastructure

## Other Functions
- Banking supervision (sole national authority)
- Fiscal agent for government debt issuance (executes bond auctions, manages settlement/clearing, operates central securities depository)
- Financial stability monitoring
- Surplus distribution to treasury: ~$80 million/year (2026 budget)

## Naming
- "Bank of Balboa" = official English name
- "Banc de Balboa" = Volisanian name (uses "de" to distinguish from "Banc Balboa" the commercial bank)
- "Central Bank of Balboa" = alternate name used in some references (e.g., Cases Justes article) — redirect created

## Gold Reserves
- 73.1 tonnes of gold managed by the Bank
- Held as part of Balboa's contribution to AC collective monetary framework
- Serves as backing asset for the International Dollar system
- Bank has custody, audit, and reporting obligations to AC monetary authorities
