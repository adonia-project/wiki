# Genètica de l'Istme — Internal Notes

## Key Facts
- **Name**: Genètica de l'Istme, S.L. (private limited company)
- **Industry**: Agricultural biotechnology — crop variety development
- **Founded**: 2023
- **Founders**: Dr. Marta Vergés i Puig (plant geneticist, CEO), Dr. Oriol Bosch i Riera (agricultural scientist, CSO)
- **Origin**: University of Sant Cristòfor spin-off from Plastic Beach
- **HQ**: Plastic Beach, Dàrsena, Sant Cristòfor
- **Employees**: ~35 (2026)
- **Facility**: ~600 m² converted warehouse (lab + greenhouse + offices)

## Ownership
- Founders: 35%
- FIDB: 20% (seed)
- Nutrició del Món (NDM): 20% (strategic — fertiliser company sees crop improvement as complement)
- University of Sant Cristòfor Investment Fund: 10% (seed)
- VC investors: 15%

## Crop Focus Areas
1. **Coffee** — disease resistance (leaf rust, berry disease) for Jala highlands cooperative farms; marker-assisted selection; field trials in Jala, results expected 2027
2. **Sugar cane & rice** — drought tolerance for Migjorn lowlands; greenhouse testing stage
3. **Cacao** — frosty pod rot + black pod disease resistance for Jala/Migjorn; early stage

## Technology
- Marker-assisted selection (not GMO — avoids regulatory/consumer issues)
- Genomic selection (predict performance before field testing)
- Local germplasm collection (Balboan varieties already adapted to local conditions)

## Key Relationships
- University of Sant Cristòfor: academic origin, graduate talent, genomic sequencing equipment
- Nutrició del Món: 20% strategic investor, complement to fertiliser business
- Cooperative farms (Jala, Migjorn): field trial partners
- CAI (Corporació Agrícola de l'Istme): field trial partner in Nurra

## Timeline
- 2023: Founded, seed funding from FIDB + USC Investment Fund + NDM strategic stake
- 2023-2026: R&D — coffee disease resistance field trials, sugar cane/rice drought tolerance greenhouse
- 2027: Expected first coffee field trial results
- Pre-commercial as of 2026
