# BioDàrsena — Internal Notes

## Key Facts
- **Name**: BioDàrsena, S.L. (private limited company)
- **Industry**: Biotechnology — industrial enzymes for plastic recycling
- **Founded**: 2021
- **Founders**: Dr. Aina Feliu i Cardona (biochemist, CEO), Dr. Bernat Oller i March (chemical engineer, CTO)
- **Origin**: University of Sant Cristòfor spin-off from the Plastic Beach biochemical research building
- **HQ**: Plastic Beach, Dàrsena, Sant Cristòfor
- **Employees**: ~25 (2026)
- **Facility**: ~800 m² converted warehouse at Plastic Beach (lab + enzyme production + pilot plant)

## Ownership
- Founders: 40%
- FIDB: 15% (early-stage institutional)
- VC investors: 45% (Zong + Illypnian syndicate)

## Technology
- Proprietary enzyme cocktail for PET biodegradation
- PETase variants (engineered for thermal stability + catalytic activity)
- Auxiliary pre-treatment enzymes (attack crystalline PET structure)
- Continuous-flow reactor (designed by Dr. Oller)
- Output: terephthalic acid + ethylene glycol → closed-loop recycling
- Lower temperature than chemical recycling → less energy

## Timeline
- 2021: Founded, seed funding from FIDB
- 2021-2024: R&D — enzyme optimization, patent filings
- 2024: Pilot plant commissioned (~2 tonnes PET/week)
- 2024-present: Exploring partnership with PDB (no formal deal yet as of 2026)

## Key Relationships

- PDB: potential industrial partner (post-consumer PET feedstock, process engineering)
