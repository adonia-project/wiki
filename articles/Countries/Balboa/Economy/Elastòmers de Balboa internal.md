# Elastòmers de Balboa — Internal Notes

## Key Facts
- **Name**: Elastòmers de Balboa, S.A. (EDB)
- **Industry**: Synthetic rubber (NBR + BR)
- **Founded**: 1991 (private), IPO 1998 on Bolsa de Sant Cristòfor (SCX: EDB)
- **Founders**: Jordi Aragó i Vidal (ex-CIEA process engineer), Marta Català i Soler (materials scientist, University of Sant Cristòfor)
- **HQ**: Sant Bart, Jala Province
- **R&D Lab**: Sant Cristòfor (Dàrsena industrial district), opened 2007
- **Manufacturing**: Badia Curta complex (adjacent to CIEA refinery + PDB resin complex)
- **Employees**: ~1,400 (1,100 Badia Curta, 100 SC lab, 200 Sant Bart HQ)
- **Revenue**: $620M (2026)
- **Operating income**: $85M
- **Net income**: $58M
- **Assets**: $780M
- **Market cap**: $640M

## Ownership
- FIDB: 8%
- BPF: 5%
- CIEA: 5% (strategic feedstock supplier)
- Public float: ~82%

## Products
- NBR (60% of revenue): low/medium/high ACN grades for automotive, industrial, gloves
- BR (30% of revenue): tyres, industrial products, golf ball cores
- Specialty blends (10%): NBR/PVC, HNBR, custom compounds

## Feedstock Chain
- Butadiene: from CIEA refinery via pipeline
- Acrylonitrile: from CIEA chemical processing unit, road tanker delivery
- Electricity: Yalabirga hydropower
- Benefits from negative tariff for exports
- Benefits from overvalued ID for cheap imported inputs (propylene, ammonia via CIEA)

## Competitive Position
- Sole domestic producer of NBR and BR in Balboa
- Replaces imports from Volisania and Breisland
- Exports to BTPMEU, Balisca, Okaiken
- Part of the Badia Curta petrochemical cluster alongside CIEA, PDB, PQQB
