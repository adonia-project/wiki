# Empresa per a Bons, Préstecs i Reemborsaments (EBPR) — Internal Notes

## Overview
- **Name**: Empresa per a Bons, Préstecs i Reemborsaments, S.A. (EBPR)
- **Translation**: "Company for Bonds, Loans, and Reimbursements"
- **Founded**: 1 May 1984, Port Soledat, Potocsí
- **Relocated**: 1992, Sant Cristòfor, Balboa
- **Ticker**: SCX: EBPR
- **Industry**: Fixed income broker-dealer and bond underwriting
- **Service area**: All of Abyala (Balboa, Potocsí, Tapuya, Balisca, Sanrobonia, Asikyira, Lacashe, Galibi)
- **Public**: Listed on Bolsa de Sant Cristòfor

## Founding Context
- Founded during the Decade Recession (Potocsí) — crisis-driven creation
- The recession (1977–1987) forced the Potocsian military junta to borrow heavily, privatise state assets, and cut fiscal transfers to municipalities
- This created demand for bond issuance across three sectors: central government (sovereign debt), state-owned enterprises (corporate bonds), and municipalities (municipal bonds)
- Traditional banking sector was focused on commercial lending, not bond underwriting
- International investment banks were reluctant to operate in Potocsí during the crisis
- A group of Potocsino investors saw the gap and founded EBPR to be a dedicated bond intermediary

## Business Lines
1. Corporate bond underwriting (oil & gas, mining, telecom, infrastructure)
2. Government bond issuance (sovereign + sub-sovereign, BTPMEU markets focus)
3. Municipal bond issuance (originated in Potocsí during recession, expanded across Abyala)
4. Bond trading and market-making (secondary market liquidity)
5. Fixed income advisory (debt structuring, liability management — newest line, est. 2010s)

## History Summary
1. **1984**: Founded in Port Soledat during Decade Recession
2. **1984–1992**: Dominant fixed income firm in Potocsí; underwrote ~60% of domestic bond issuances
3. **1992**: Relocated HQ to Sant Cristòfor (Districte Financer, 180 Carrer del Comerç)
4. **Mid-1990s**: Expanded into Tapuya (municipal bonds)
5. **1996**: Listed on Bolsa de Sant Cristòfor
6. **Late 1990s–early 2000s**: Expanded into Balisca and Sanrobonia
7. **2010s**: Developed fixed income advisory division
8. **2020**: Pandemic surge in bond issuance reinforced market position
9. **2026**: Largest dedicated fixed income firm in Abyala

## Relationship to Other Potocsí-founded Companies
- EBPR is the third Potocsí-founded company on the Bolsa de Sant Cristòfor
- RDM (Recursos del Marañón) relocated 1977
- AUA (Assegurances Unificades d'Abyala) relocated 1979
- EBPR relocated 1992 — later than the others because it was founded later (1984) and was not fleeing political persecution, but rather seeking strategic growth
