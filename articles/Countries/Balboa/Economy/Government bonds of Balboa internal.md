## Key decisions (confirmed by user)
- **Article title**: "Government bonds of Balboa"
- **Issuer management**: Split roles — Ministry of Finance decides policy (how much to borrow, maturity mix); Bank of Balboa executes auctions and handles settlement
- **Sub-sovereign issuance**: All three levels can issue — provinces, comarcas, and municipalities
- **Instruments**: Full range — T-bills (<1yr), notes (2-10yr), bonds (10-30yr), inflation-linked bonds, infrastructure bonds

## Invented names/terms
- **Tresoreria** (Treasury division) — office within Ministry of Finance, established 1993
- **Lletres del Tresor** — Treasury bills (short-term, <1yr)
- **Bons del Tresor** — Treasury notes (medium-term, 2-10yr)
- **Obligacions del Tresor** — Treasury bonds (long-term, 10-30yr)
- **Bons del Tresor indexats** — Inflation-linked bonds
- **Bons d'infraestructura** — Infrastructure bonds (already referenced in budget)
- **Primary dealer system** — established 1993, includes major banks + EBPR
- **Quarterly issuance calendar** — published by Ministry of Finance

## Invented institutional details
- Tresoreria established 1993 as part of fiscal reforms (LVT same year)
- ACMF recommended dedicated treasury function post-1994
- Primary dealer system established 1993
- Primary dealers: Banc Balboa, Banc de l'Istme, Banc de Jala, CBNM, SY Balboa, EBPR
- Inflation-linked programme introduced early 2000s
- Municipal bond market expanded in 1990s with fiscal decentralisation
- Sant Cristòfor municipal bond programme is largest sub-sovereign programme

## Naming convention notes
- "Bank of Balboa" = central bank (English name, used in Budget article)
- "Banc Balboa" = commercial bank (Volisanian name, involved in real-estate crisis)
- "Central Bank of Balboa" = same as Bank of Balboa (used in Cases Justes article)
- Volisanian instrument names follow Catalan convention: Lletres (bills), Bons (notes), Obligacions (bonds)
- "Tresoreria" = Treasury division within Ministry of Finance
