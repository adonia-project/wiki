## Key Facts
- **Company**: Sistemes OrSolar, S.L. (commonly: OrSolar)
- **Type**: Private limited company (S.L.)
- **Industry**: Artificial intelligence
- **Founded**: 2023
- **Founders**: Abe Zayna (CEO) and Ryuko Vidal (CTO) — both Balboan, from Sant Cristòfor, both PhD researchers who worked at Kizan Systems in Okaiken before returning to Balboa
- **Location**: Plastic Beach, Metallúrgia Coastal Research Cluster, Dàrsena, Sant Cristòfor

- **Product**: XatXat 1.0 — first domestic LLM in Balboa, released 31 August 2026
  - General-purpose text LLM
  - Competes with major models on cheaper benchmarks
  - Mobile app released same day (chatbot interface)
  - XatPro subscription: $5/month pro plan with enhanced capabilities
  - Mission: affordable AI for the rest of Adonia
- **Valuation**: $5 billion (announced on launch day, 31 August 2026)
- **Employees**: 300 (2026)
- **Controversy**: Accused of distilling model training from Kizan Systems' Satori AI
  - Both founders worked at Kizan Systems on Satori before leaving
  - OrSolar denies allegations
  - No formal legal action as of Aug 2026
