### Historical narrative (invented)
- 1998 founding context: linked to Banc Balboa's forced divestiture from Grup Financer Central (Volisanian) — this event is documented in the Banc Balboa article
- PayCard as the dominant global network (from unorganized/PayCard.mediawiki) — TpA positioned as regional alternative
- Early operations: launched Dec 1999, limited to Sant Cristòfor/Estret corridor + Port Soledat
- Expansion timeline: Tapuya & Asikyira (2001), Guajicutea (2005), Lacashe (2007)
- IPO: June 2007 on Bolsa de Sant Cristòfor
- Technology: EMV chip-and-PIN (2011-2015), contactless (2014-2017), mobile wallet (2016)
- Secondary data centre in Port Soledat, Potocsí (2022)
- Pandemic boost: 40% increase in transaction volume 2020-2021

### Card products
- Credit cards (primary, since 1999)
- Debit cards (since 2004)
- Prepaid cards (since 2009)

### Other invented details
- ~40 member banks total as of 2026
- First CEO Pere Amat i Solà recruited from Bolsa de Sant Cristòfor
- TpA does not issue cards directly — licenses brand/network to member banks
- Originally cooperative, transitioned to public company in 2007 while retaining member-bank governance
- Secondary processing facility in Port Soledat for geographic redundancy
