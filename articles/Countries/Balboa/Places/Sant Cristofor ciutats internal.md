## Geography notes

- **Bay side (west)**: Original (1), Barcelona (6) — face the Badia de Sant Cristòfor
- **Shendan coast (southwest)**: Dàrsena (7), Mare de Déu del Mar (9) — open Shendan Ocean coastline
- **Inland/east**: Sant Miquel (3), Sant Jordi (4), Ciutat del Pujol (5) — urban core extending east toward hills
- **North**: Costa Nord (2) — northern suburbs, border with Els Aiguamolls
- **South**: Ciutat de l'Estació (11) — canal corridor, railyards
- **South-central**: Sant Martí (8), Sant Llorenç (10) — suburban, inland

## Ciutat–districte system

Sant Cristòfor is the only city in Balboa to operate a ciutat–districte system. Each ciutat has its own elected city council (consell de ciutat), and each ciutat is further subdivided into districtes, each electing a representative to its city council. The Municipalitat de les Illes (islands) is not part of the ciutat structure but operates as a distinct municipalitat within the same comarca.
