# Els Aiguamolls (comarca) — Internal Notes

## Overview
- Comarca 4 of Estret Province, Balboa
- Shendan coast, immediately north of Sant Cristòfor comarca
- Population: 1,665,000 (2026) — third most populous comarca in Estret
- Area: 679 km² — density 2,452/km² (highest in Estret Province)
- Capital: Vilanova (374,420)
- Character: coastal wetlands + dense suburban bedroom community for Sant Cristòfor
- 15 municipalities (13 mainland + 3 island)

## Geographic Layout (centroids)
- **Southern (near Sant Cristòfor)**: Sant Joan (SE), La Riba (S coast), Les Salines (SW coast), Els Canyars (S-central)
- **Central**: Vilanova (capital, central), Sant Mateu (W-central), Montclar (E, near Serralada)
- **Northern**: Sant Cugat (N-central, airport), Vallclara (NE), Sant Antoni (N), El Puig (NE), La Llacuna (NW)
- **Islands (offshore NW)**: Illa dels Ocells (S, closest to mainland), Sant Miquel (W, largest), Illa de Fora (N, farthest)

## Borders
- South: Riu Llobregat (bordering Sant Cristòfor comarca)
- North: Les Planes comarca
- East: Serralada d'Estret (bordering L'Estany comarca)
- West: Shendan Ocean

## Transport
- CristoCarril Line E: 3 stations in comarca (Aiguamolls Sud, Els Canyars, La Llacuna terminus)
- E1 expressway: principal north-south route through eastern comarca
- E4 expressway: cross-isthmus link through southeastern comarca (near Montclar)
- Sant Cristòfor Els Aiguamolls Airport (EAI): in Sant Cugat municipality, 80,000 pax (2025)
- La Llacuna station has shuttle to L'Estany Airport (neighbouring comarca)
