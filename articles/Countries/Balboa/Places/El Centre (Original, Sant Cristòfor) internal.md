## Key Corrections (2026-09-02)
- El Centre is NOT a waterfront barri — the Districte Financer sits between El Centre and the bay
- Districte Financer was carved out of El Centre in the late 1980s with the rise of high-rise office towers along the waterfront
- The Embarcadero (waterfront boulevard) runs through the Districte Financer, NOT El Centre
- The Torre del Comerç (1931, Zong bank HQ) was originally in El Centre but now falls within the Districte Financer after the 1980s split
- The Torre del Comerciant (2002, 222m) and the Bolsa de Sant Cristòfor (39 Carretera de Pedro González) are both in El Centre (inland portion)
- The Bank of Balboa (1400 Avinguda Llibertat) is in El Centre

## GIS Data
- El Centre bounds: (-94.395, 0.619, -94.377, 0.639)
- Districte Financer bounds: (-94.403, 0.626, -94.391, 0.641)
- Districte Financer is west of El Centre (further west = closer to bay)
- El Centre neighbors: Districte Financer (west), Plaça Major (west/southwest), Barri del Te (north), La Garriga (south/east)
- El Centre is the 9th most western barri — set back from the bay

## Demographics
- Population: 33,181

## Transport
- No metro station (one of three barris without metro: El Centre, La Sagrada, Les Vil·les)
- T3 streetcar line along Avinguda Llibertat
- 24-hour bus service (particularly important for barris without metro)
