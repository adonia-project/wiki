# La Serra de Llevant — Internal Notes

## Key Facts
- **Name**: La Serra de Llevant ("Eastern Mountain Range")
- **Type**: Comarca (second-level administrative division)
- **Province**: Tramuntana Province
- **Capital**: Vila de Llevant (pop 8,532) — relocated 1970 from original lakeside site
- **Largest settlement**: Sant Jorge (pop 12,204) — founded 1625 as Volisanian trading post
- **Population**: 29,729 (2026 estimate)
- **Geographic features**: Lake Iwarakí (704.586 km² reservoir), Gran Presa del Nord dam, Yalabirga Mountain Range, Birú sacred caves (limestone formations on eastern slope)
- **Transport**: E8 expressway passes through Sant Jorge; Sant Jorge Airport (SJD/SJIB, turboprop-only, 55K pax 2025)
- **Borders**: La Taula comarca (NW), Tramuntana Province interior (W/S), Nurra Province (E)

## Context from Existing Lore
- Tramuntana is the poorest province in Balboa ($7B GDP, $2,400 per capita, 1.9M population)
- Provincial capital: Portblanc (pop. 109,965)
- Birú indigenous people concentrated in remote highland comarcas of Jala and Tramuntana
- La Serra de Llevant is the easternmost comarca in Tramuntana Province
- Sant Jorge was comarca capital until 1970, when capital moved to relocated Vila de Llevant
- Gran Presa del Nord: constructed 1970–1979, CHB-operated, on Lake Iwarakí
- 2021 Carretera Gran Presa del Nord protest: March 8, 2021, sit-in against road tunnel through Birú sacred caves, Hernandez Lee kidnapped and killed by NdS guard Jonaes Mendosas i Vidal
- 2026 data center protests: NTE broke ground near Sant Jorge on Aug 18, 2026, triggering nationwide protests (Aug 18–27), construction halted
- Comarca name means "Eastern Mountain Range" in Volisanian — refers to position on eastern highlands of Tramuntana
