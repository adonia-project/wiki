# Plastic Beach — Internal Notes

## Key Facts
- **Name**: Plastic Beach (Platja de Plàstic) — informal name adopted in common usage
- **Official name**: Metallúrgia Coastal Research Cluster (Clúster d'Investigació Costaner de Metallúrgia)
- **Location**: Metallúrgia neighbourhood, Dàrsena ciutat, Sant Cristòfor, Estret Province
- **Site**: Former Dàrsena metalworks (1910–1934), steel and heavy engineering complex
- **Area**: ~0.8 km² on the southern coast of Dàrsena, along Badia de Sant Cristòfor
- **Established as cluster**: c. 2018 (municipal designation)
- **Population/employees**: ~3,200 (2026)
- **Companies**: ~25 companies and research groups

## Three Sectors
1. **Plastics/polymers**: PDB (research division, ~180 staff), EDB (R&D lab, ~100 staff), CPJ (biodegradable plastics pilot, ~40 staff)
2. **Biotechnology**: BioDàrsena (industrial enzymes, ~25 staff), Genètica de l'Istme (agri biotech, ~35 staff), Laboratoris Costa de Dàrsena (contract research, ~60 staff)
3. **Chemical research**: PQQB (applied research, ~50 staff), CQO (pilot plant, ~30 staff), GQC (formulation lab, ~20 staff)

## History
- 1910: Dàrsena metalworks established (blast furnaces, foundries, structural steel)
- 1920s: Peak employment ~3,000 workers
- 1934: Metalworks permanently closed (never recovered from GAW damage)
- 1934–2010s: Site largely abandoned, neighbourhood declined into low-income residential
- 1980s–1990s: Northern Dàrsena gentrified but Metallúrgia bypassed (distance from Metro, contaminated land, no waterfront access)
- 2018: Municipal Council designated area as research/technology zone with tax incentives
- 2019: PDB relocated research division to converted warehouse
- 2020: EDB established R&D lab in cluster
- 2020: Official designation as Metallúrgia Coastal Research Cluster
- 2022–2023: More companies moved in (biotech startups, chemical research labs)
- 2026: ~25 companies, ~3,200 employees, 3 office towers under construction

## New Construction (2026–2029)
- Metallúrgia Tower (22 storeys, 2027) — 18,000 m² office/lab
- Platja Tower (18 storeys, 2028) — biotech/pharma focused
- Dàrsena Coast Tower (28 storeys, 2029) — largest, consortium with FIDB

## Development Drivers
- Municipal tax incentives (Dàrsena Council, 2018)
- Provincial redevelopment grants (land remediation, warehouse conversion)
- FIDB infrastructure investment (shared lab facility, power/data)
- University of Sant Cristòfor research partnership programme
