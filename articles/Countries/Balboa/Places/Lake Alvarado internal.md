# Lake Alvarado — Internal Development Notes

## Core facts (confirmed by user 2026-09-16)

- **Surface area**: 128.788 km² (the 420 km² figure previously in the Balboa Canal article was WRONG and has been corrected)
- **Origin**: dredged from the Alvarado wetlands, NOT dammed — no highland-basin dam
- **Elevation**: around sea level — NOT the 26 m previously stated
- **Outflow**: Rio Alvarado, western outlet to the Shendan Ocean; "naturally" connected, no locks on the west side
- **Hydrology**: rain-fed; no major inflowing rivers
- **Shoreline**: urbanized essentially all the way around
- **Etymology**: named after [[Miguel Alvarado]], first viceroy of the Viceroyalty of Balboa (no other details on him yet — no article exists)
- **Depths** (agent-proposed, accepted implicitly — flag for user confirmation): average ~5.5 m; natural basin max ~9 m; dredged navigation channel 16 m
- **Volume**: ~0.7 km³ (derived from area × average depth)

## Shoreline geography (clockwise from 12 o'clock, confirmed by user)

1. **Areza** (north shore) — International City
2. Suburban/rural comarcas of **Migjorn** (east/southeast)
3. **Les Dàrsenes** (comarca, Migjorn)
4. **L'Espigó** (comarca, Migjorn) — south of the Rio Alvarado
5. **Portnou** (west) — north of the Rio Alvarado; capital of Marjal del Canal (Estret)

- Rio Alvarado splits Portnou (north bank) from L'Espigó (south bank)
- Lake straddles the Estret–Migjorn provincial boundary

## Canal profile (revised 2026-09-16, updated 2026-09-17)

- Full route: Shendan Ocean → Rio Alvarado (channelized) → Lake Alvarado (sea level, no locks) → Les Dàrsenes Locks (1.286 km reach, northeast) → Lake Les Dàrsenes (26 m) → Mario Locks (1.823 km reach) → Lake Mario (30 m, SUMMIT) → Aethiopian Locks → Sant Llàtzer (Aethiopian, sea level)
- **Lake Les Dàrsenes**: artificial, dammed, 26 m elevation, area 28.941 km²
- **Lake Mario**: artificial, dammed, 30 m elevation (summit level), area 2.123 km²
- **No Shendan Locks** — the western side is entirely at sea level
- Old "Alvarado Locks" renamed → **Les Dàrsenes Locks** (at the northeast, between Lake Alvarado and Lake Les Dàrsenes)
- Lock sets: 3 sets / 6 chambers total (Les Dàrsenes, Mario, Aethiopian) — 2 chambers per set (agent-proposed, user accepted 2026-09-17)
- Lock reach distances are TOTAL reach lengths (lock flight + approach channels): 1.286 km (Alvarado→Les Dàrsenes), 1.823 km (Les Dàrsenes→Mario)
