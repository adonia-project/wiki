## Geography notes

- **Bay side (west)**: Barris along the Badia de Sant Cristòfor waterfront — ferry terminals, harbour, promenades
- **Southern edge**: Transitional zone toward Dàrsena — historically working-class, industrial fringe
- **Central core**: Historic centre, government, financial district, main railway station
- **Eastern edge**: Inward-facing, toward the canal corridor and eastern suburbs
- **Northern edge**: Transition toward Costa Nord

### Ethnic composition notes (narrative)

- **Black Balboans** (35.2%): Majority in southern/working-class barris — El Raval (75%), La Tanneria (72%), Districte Càrnic (68%), Districte Càrnic Sud (65%), La Verneda (52%). Also significant in L'Eixample Est (42%) and Mercat Plaça (48%).
- **Mixed Balboans** (19.3%): Distributed throughout; highest in Els Magatzems (26%), La Prosperitat (28%), L'Eixample Sud (25%).
- **Sinchew Balboans** (9.0%): Concentrated in L'Eixample Est (15%), Barri dels Teatres (20%), Barri del Te (12%), Turó del Temple (12%) — the former Districte Est.
- **White Balboans** (8.0%): Concentrated in wealthy northern barris — Les Vil·les (38%), La Seu (30%), La Diagonal (28%), Els Jardins (25%), Districte Financer (25%). Also significant in El Centre (16%) and Plaça Major (18%).
- **Zong Balboans** (8.0%): Concentrated in eastern barris — Barri del Te (38%), Turó del Temple (35%), Barri dels Teatres (18%). Also present in El Centre (10%), Plaça Major (10%), Districte Financer (12%).
- **Nakamian Balboans** (4.4%): Overwhelmingly concentrated in Petit Nakamizu (58% — the Nakamian cultural heart). Also present in Districte Financer (8%), La Diagonal (8%), Turó del Temple (8%), Barri del Te (8%).
- **Other** (16.1%): Immigrants, international students (especially La Universitat 36%, La Seu 39%), and smaller communities.

## Metro stations

7 of the city's 9 metro lines serve Original (Lines 1–7; Line 8 clips the southeast corner only; Line 9 does not serve Original). There are **29 stations** in Original, including 6 major hubs (3+ lines) and 8 minor hubs (2 lines). 3 barris — El Centre, La Sagrada, Les Vil·les — have no metro station and are served by streetcar (T1–T4) and bus.

## Barri naming conventions

Most barris use Catalan-language names following the pattern established by the city's Volisanian colonial and Zong-period heritage. Names typically reference:
- Geographic features (rivers, hills, coast)
- Historical functions (markets, gates, ports)
- Patron saints or religious sites
- Former land use (orchards, quarries, marshes)
- Former towns or villages absorbed into the city
