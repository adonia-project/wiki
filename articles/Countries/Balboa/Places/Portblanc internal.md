## User-Provided Details (2026-08-31)
- **Founding**: Discovered by Volisanian explorers in 1526 (small Birú fishing village). Mission established 1568. Made provincial capital of Tramuntana in the Viceroyalty of Balboa.
- **Etymology**: "White Port" (Volisanian *port blanc*) — named for white cliffs at harbour entrance
- **Demonym**: Portblanquí (m), Portblanquina (f)
- **Military Academy**: Established in early rump state period (1900s–1910s) — chose 1908
- **Climate**: Tropical savanna
- **University**: Yes, in Portblanc — Universitat de Portblanc
- **Streetcar**: Invent details — created 3-line system (1934 original, 1952 expansion, 1998 modernisation)
- **City area**: ~250 km²
- **Elevation**: 5–200m (city stretches from harbour at sea level into hills of coastal Tramuntana range)
- **Coastal Tramuntana range**: Small elevated range on the Tramuntana coast, separate from and west of the main Yalabirga highlands. Portblanc extends from the coast into these hills.
- **Government**: City council
- **Port**: Invent details — regional cargo, fishing harbour, rump state western port
