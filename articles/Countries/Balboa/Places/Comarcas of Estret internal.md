## Comarca 1 — Marjal del Canal

**Name**: Marjal del Canal (Volisanian: "Canal Marshes" — named for the wetland/marsh area around the canal and Lake Alvarado)

**Capital**: Portnou — the Shendan terminus of the Balboa Canal

**Character**: Canal/port economy + industrial. The comarca is dominated by the canal infrastructure, port facilities, shipping, and associated industrial zones (warehouses, logistics, manufacturing). Portnou is the primary city.

**Borders**:
- South: The Balboa Canal and Lake Alvarado
- East: Areza (the International City, sits between comarcas 1, 2, and 13; Lake Alvarado is south of Areza)
- Northeast: Lake Jarumá (natural lake at the NE corner of the comarca)
- West: Shendan coast
- NW: Riu Burutí (river forming the border with comarca 2, flows to the Shendan)

**Geography**: Southernmost Shendan-coast comarca. Borders Lake Alvarado and the canal to the south, Lake Jarumá to the northeast, and the Riu Burutí to the west. Centroid at (-94.38, -0.02). Area ~0.082 (medium-small).

**Indigenous place names**:
- **Riu Burutí**: From Birú *burutí* ("muddy water"), bastardized into Volisanian as "Burutí"
- **Lake Jarumá**: From Birú *yarumá* ("still water"), bastardized into Volisanian as "Jarumá" (Volisanian: Estany de Jarumá)

**Open questions**:
- Other towns besides Portnou?

**Historical notes**: Originally marshland with small Birú fishing settlements. The canal construction transformed it into an industrial/port zone. Before the canal, Portnou was just a small coastal village.

**Population**: ~1,450,000 (2026 est.) | Area: 1,012 km² | Density: 1,433/km²

## Comarca 2 — Cumará

**Name**: Cumará (from Birú *kumara* = "resting place", bastardized into Volisanian)

**Capital**: Varakí (from Birú *warakí* = "peaceful water", bastardized into Volisanian)

**Character**: Suburban/residential — bedroom community for Sant Cristòfor. Commuter towns along the Shendan coast between the Burutí river and the city.

**Borders**:
- West: Shendan coast
- Southeast: Riu Burutí (border with comarca 1)
- South: Lake Alvarado + tiny sliver of Areza
- Southeast: Serralada d'Estret (Estret Hills — the Yalabirga Mountain Range at its lowest point here, ~250m max, border with comarca 13)
- North: Riu Besòs (border with comarca 3 / Sant Cristòfor)

**Geography**: Central-south, Shendan coast. Centroid at (-94.31, +0.22). Area ~0.103.

**Open questions**:
- Other towns besides Varakí?

**Note**: The southern third of the comarca is effectively continuous Sant Cristòfor suburbia; the northern two-thirds is more distinct commuter towns.

**Population**: ~2,550,000 (2026 est.) | Area: 1,272 km² | Density: 2,006/km²

## Comarca 3 — Sant Cristòfor

**Name**: Sant Cristòfor

**Capital**: Sant Cristòfor (also the provincial capital of Estret)

**Character**: Cosmopolitan, metro area — financial centre, free port, cultural hub

**Borders**:
- West: Shendan coast (Bay of Sant Cristòfor)
- South: Riu Besòs (border with comarca 2)
- East: Serralada d'Estret (Estret Hills, border with comarca 13)
- North: Riu Llobregat (border with comarcas 4 and 5)

**Geography**: Central coast, Shendan side. The comarca includes the **Badia de Sant Cristòfor** (Bay of Sant Cristòfor) and the **Península de la Barceloneta**. Centroid at (-94.27, +0.54). Area ~0.213 (2nd largest comarca).

**Islands** (3 major islands in the Bay of Sant Cristòfor):

| Illa de Matarí | 6.365 km² | Oval | Park; flat, marshy | Birú *matarí* ("wet land"), bastardized into Volisanian |

## Comarca 4 — Els Aiguamolls

**Name**: Els Aiguamolls (Volisanian: "The Wetlands")

**Capital**: Vilanova (Volisanian: "New Town" — suburban centre)

**Character**: Extensive coastal wetlands and marshes along the Shendan coast; suburban residential inland. Manages four uninhabited islands offshore.

**Borders**:
- West: Shendan coast (wetlands/marshes)
- East: Serralada d'Estret (border with Aethiopian-side comarcas)
- South: Riu Llobregat (border with comarca 3 / Sant Cristòfor)
- North: Arbitrary boundary with comarca 5 (Les Planes) — split due to political representation differences

**Geography**: North-central, Shendan coast. Centroid at (-94.31, +0.89). Area ~0.055 (small).

**Islands** (4 uninhabited islands off the Shendan coast):

| Island | Area | Shape/Character | Name origin |

## Comarca 5 — Les Planes

**Name**: Les Planes (Volisanian: "The Plains" — flat agricultural land between the coast and the hills)

**Capital**: Sant Vicenç (Volisanian saint name, common for small towns)

**Character**: Exurban, agricultural, and fishing. Small comarca with a mix of coastal fishing villages, inland farms, and exurban sprawl from the Sant Cristòfor metro area.

**Borders**:
- West: Shendan coast
- East: Serralada d'Estret (border with Aethiopian-side comarcas)
- South: Arbitrary boundary with comarca 4 (Els Aiguamolls) — split due to 5 wanting different representation from the purely suburban 4)
- North: Riu Segre (border with comarca 6) — originates from Estany de Panacú

**Geography**: North, Shendan coast. Centroid at (-94.23, +1.07). Area ~0.042 (smallest comarca).

**Historical note**: Comarca 5 was split from comarca 4 along an arbitrary line because the rural communities of 5 wanted different political representation from the purely suburban 4.

**Population**: ~440,000 (2026 est.) | Area: 520 km² | Density: 847/km²

**Open questions**:
- Other towns besides Sant Vicenç?

## Comarca 9 — La Marina

**Name**: La Marina (Volisanian: "The Navy" — named for the naval presence)

**Capital**: Haicang (Hokkien 海港 *Hái-káng*, "sea port")

**Character**: Naval/military, ship repair. Mid-sized city founded as a Zong Balboa military base, now home to the Balboan navy station and ship repair yards. Sits on the Badia del Ferro (Iron Bay).

**Borders**:
- East: Aethiopian coast (Badia del Ferro)
- West: Serralada d'Estret (border with comarca 8)
- South: Riu Ebre (border with comarca 10)
- North: Province border (northern edge of Estret)

**Geography**: NE, Aethiopian coast. Centroid at (-93.34, +1.24). Area ~0.118.

**Key features**:
- **Badia del Ferro** (Volisanian: "Iron Bay") — the bay on the Aethiopian coast
- **Haicang** — mid-sized city, Zong-era military base, now naval station + ship repair
- Balboan navy station

**Population**: ~580,000 (2026 est.) | Area: 1,449 km² | Density: 400/km²

**Open questions**:
- Other towns besides Haicang?

## Comarca 11 — L'Estany

**Name**: L'Estany (Volisanian: "The Lake" — named for the large lake at its centre)

**Capital**: Bellver (Volisanian: "beautiful view" — overlooking the lake)

**Character**: Lakeside tourism hub. The south shore has wealthy enclaves and resort communities; the north shore has light industry, fishing, and agriculture. The only inland comarca in Estret.

**Borders**:
- West: Ridgeline of the Panacú basin (border with Shendan-side comarcas 4, 5, 6)
- East: Ridgeline of the Panacú basin (border with Aethiopian-side comarcas 10, 13)
- South: Ridgeline of the Panacú basin (border with comarca 13)
- North: Ridgeline of the Panacú basin (border with comarca 10)

**Note**: The comarca is defined by the natural basin/depression containing the Estany de Panacú. Its borders with all surrounding comarcas follow the ridgeline around the basin.

**Geography**: Central-north, inland. Centroid at (-93.99, +0.92). Area ~0.116.

**Key features**:
- **Estany de Panacú** — large natural lake at the centre of the comarca (from Birú *panakú* = "great basin", bastardized into Volisanian)
- River originating from the lake flows west through the Serralada d'Estret to the Shendan coast (forms border between comarcas 5 and 6)

**Population**: ~195,000 (2026 est.) | Area: 1,427 km² | Density: 137/km²

**Open questions**:
- Other towns besides Bellver?

## Comarca 12 — Illes del Guano

**Name**: Illes del Guano (Volisanian: "Guano Islands")

**Capital**: Cala Llarga (Volisanian: "Long Cove" — the largest fishing village, on island A)

**Character**: Largely uninhabited archipelago off the Aethiopian coast. Small fishing villages scattered across the islands. Formerly home to extensive guano mining operations.

**Borders**:
- Surrounded by the Aethiopian Ocean
- West: Comarca 10 (Llevant) or 13 (nearest mainland comarca)

**Geography**: Off the Aethiopian coast. Centroid at (-93.22, +0.63). Area ~0.058.

**Islands** (5 main islands, mixed naming):

| Island | Area | Name | Name origin |
|--------|------|------|-------------|
| A | 562.151 km² | Illa de Tamaní | Birú *tamaní* ("island place") — largest island |
| B | 125.536 km² | Illa del Fer | Volisanian "Iron Island" — former mining infrastructure |
| C | 35.457 km² | Illa de Karawí | Birú *karawí* ("lonely rock") |
| D | 35.176 km² | Illa dels Ocells | Volisanian "Bird Island" — guano birds |
| E | 11.588 km² | Illa de Burutí | Birú *burutí* ("muddy water") |

**Population**: ~8,500 (2026 est.) | Area: 711 km² | Density: 12/km²

**Open questions**:
- Is guano mining completely defunct or still ongoing at small scale?

**Note**: Guano mining was extensive historically but is now largely defunct. The islands are mostly nature reserves. Administratively falls under the Aethiopian coast jurisdiction, closest to comarca 10 (Llevant).
