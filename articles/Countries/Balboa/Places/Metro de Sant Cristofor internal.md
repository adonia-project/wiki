## Key facts

- 9 lines, 362.4 km total
- ~1.2 million passengers daily
- Lines 1 and 2: Zong-era, originally only went up to Original's borders, since extended
- Line 5: goes south and around the bay (Barcelona → Original, the long way — no cross-bay metro)
- Line 9: connects to Areza Metro at southern terminus
- Lines 8 and 9: extend into Cumará comarca

## Hubs

- **Original**: 7 of 9 lines converge (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7) — the central hub
- **Dàrsena**: 6 lines (2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8) — secondary hub

## Ciutats with no metro

- **Ciutat del Pujol** (ID 5): no metro — elite hillside, car-dependent, low density
- **Mare de Déu del Mar** (ID 9): only Line 4 — wealthy coastal, low density
