# Time in Balboa — Internal Notes

## Key Decisions
- **Abbreviation**: BST (Balboa Standard Time) — confirmed by user 2026-09-02. Changed from BQT to BST per user request.
- **UTC offset**: UTC−05:00 — established from Portblanc article
- **DST**: None — confirmed by user. Country is near equator (~6-9°N), minimal seasonal daylight variation.
- **Single zone**: Entire country uses one time zone — confirmed by user.
- **Adoption**: Post-independence (after 15 March 1854) — confirmed by user. Part of broader administrative standardisation.
- **Date format**: dd/mm/yyyy — inferred from Volisanian-language conventions (same as Burawa). Not explicitly confirmed by user.
- **IANA identifier**: Abyala/Balboa — confirmed by user 2026-09-02.
