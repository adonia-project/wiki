# Alcántara Corrent — Internal Notes

## Overview
EMUs manufactured by Alcántara Corporation (Balisca) for Intercon's CristoCarril commuter rail network. Entered service 2011. Now used by most EMU Balboa railroad operators for commuter rail.

## Key Facts
- **Model name**: Alcántara Corrent (Baliscan for "current/flow")
- **Class designation**: Class CC-100 (Intercon), Class RDP-100 (RdP), Class FMS-100 (FMS)
- **Type**: Electric Multiple Unit (EMU)
- **Formation**: 4, 5, or 6-car semi-permanently coupled trainset
  - 4-car: DM–M–M–DM (~84m, 520 pax)
  - 5-car: DM–M–T–M–DM (~105m, 650 pax)
  - 6-car: DM–M–T–M–M–DM (~125m, 780 pax)
- **Manufacturer**: Alcántara, Merced Works, Balisca
- **Service entry**: 2011
- **Total built**: 112 (all operators)

## Design Details (from user description)
- **Body**: Lightweight, streamlined; largely rectangular passenger compartment with rounded cab at each end
- **Livery**: Silver and light grey with blue and turquoise accent panels extending around cab and along sides
- **Cab**: Rounded, dark-tinted windscreen in black front fascia; small headlights at lower corners; electronic destination display above windscreen; cab sides taper slightly toward front
- **Doors**: 2 double-leaf sliding doors per side per car (4 doors total per car), positioned in outer thirds of carbody
- **Windows**: Large rectangular, dark continuous glazing frames forming horizontal band along upper half; door glazing narrower and more vertical
- **Roof**: Prominent rooftop equipment housings; overhead catenary collection via pantograph
- **Bogies**: Conventional (not Jacobs)
- **Coupling**: Semi-permanent within trainset; Scharfenberg between trainsets
- **Multiple working**: Up to 2 trainsets (max 12 cars)

## Procurement
- Intercon issued tender 2007
- Two bidders: Alcántara (Balisca) vs Okami Heavy Industries (Okaiken)
- Alcántara won due to:
  1. Established commuter EMU portfolio (vs Okami's heavy/high-speed rail focus)
  2. Balisca-Balboa trade proximity and established relationship
  3. Technology transfer offer + local maintenance support
  4. Lower cost (shorter supply chain vs Shendan Ocean shipping)
- Contract awarded 2009

## Technical Specs
- Max speed: 140 km/h
- Power: 1,200 kW (4-car), 1,500 kW (5-car), 1,800 kW (6-car)
- Gauge: 1,435 mm standard
- Electrification: 25 kV 50 Hz AC overhead
- Brakes: Regenerative + electro-pneumatic
- Seating: Transverse 2+2
- HVAC: Yes
- Floor height: ~1,100 mm (near-level boarding)
