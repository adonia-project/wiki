# Coll Alt (CristoCarril) — Internal Notes

## Overview
- CristoCarril station on Line F, station #12 of 13
- Highest elevated station in the CristoCarril system (~185 m)
- Located at mountain pass in Serralada d'Estret foothills
- Coordinates: (-94.06, 0.58) = 0°34'48"N, 94°3'36"W
- In Sant Jordi municipality, Sant Cristòfor comarca, Estret Province
- km 52.6 from Sant Cristòfor Central Terminal
- ~300 daily boardings (lowest of any CC station)

## Key Details
- Opened: 11 April 1915
- Originally a freight terminus for a copper mine at the mountain pass
- Tunnel constructed Jan 8, 1913 – Jan 8, 1915 (2 years); station opened 3 months later
- Was the end of the line until passenger service through tunnel to L'Estany started (Jun 18, 1920)
- After passenger service extension, became a through station on Line F
- 2 side platforms, 2 tracks
- Station code: CAL
- Only Line F local service stops here (express bypasses)
- 2-car train formations only due to switchback constraints

## History (invented details)
- Mine extracted copper ore from Serralada d'Estret deposits (consistent with Yalabirga copper mining)
- Railway built to transport ore down switchbacks to coastal plain
- Tunnel built simultaneously (1913-1915) to extend corridor through ridge
- Small mining settlement developed around station
- Mine eventually depleted, freight operations ceased
- Corridor repurposed for passenger service as part of CristoCarril network
- Remaining track to L'Estany laid by 1920; passenger service started Jun 18, 1920
