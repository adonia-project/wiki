# Sant Cristòfor–L'Estany Rail Tunnel — Internal Notes

## Overview
- Rail tunnel on the Old Grand Subdivision, Estret Province, Balboa
- Carries CristoCarril Line F through the Serralada d'Estret
- ~8.2 km long (estimated — longest rail tunnel on CristoCarril network)
- Single bore, single track
- Western portal: near Coll Alt station (~185 m elevation)
- Eastern portal: interior side of ridge (~120 m elevation)
- Gentle descending grade west to east

## Key Dates
- Construction start: 8 January 1913
- Construction end: 8 January 1915 (2 years)
- Coll Alt station opened: 11 April 1915 (3 months after tunnel completion)
- Passenger service through tunnel to L'Estany: 18 June 1920
- Tunnel initially freight-only (copper mine); passenger service started when remaining track to L'Estany was laid

## Design
- Bored from both ends simultaneously (2 headings)
- Geology: weathered sedimentary rock (relatively easy boring)
- Serralada d'Estret max elevation: 250 m
- Tunnel electrified: 25 kV AC overhead
- Max speed: 80 km/h (same as rest of Line F)
- Single track — signalling controls bidirectional movements

## Context
- Built as part of the Old Grand Subdivision freight railway
- Copper mine at Coll Alt was the primary freight customer
- Mine eventually depleted; corridor repurposed for passenger service
- Track between eastern portal and L'Estany laid by 1920
- L'Estany is on the Estany de Panacú, in Ciutat del Llac Sud
