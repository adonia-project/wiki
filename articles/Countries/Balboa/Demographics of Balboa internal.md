# Demographics of Balboa — Internal Notes

## Quick Reference
- Population: 35,234,000 (2026)
- Density: 119/km²
- Growth rate: 0.8% (2025)
- Urbanisation: 72%
- Median age: 29.4
- Fertility rate: 2.1 (near replacement)
- Life expectancy: 76.8 years (Estret 79.2, Tramuntana 72.4)
- Infant mortality: 14/1,000
- Literacy: 95%

## Migration
- **Redilution/Birthing** (1854–1890): ~200K Illypnian settlers; white pop 15%→30%
- **Zong Balboa** (1899–1950): Fosian immigration (Zong, Sinchewese)
- **Post-reunification**: Modest Kaftian + Lurandian immigration
- **GAW**: ~1/3 displaced; Zong elite emigrated
- **Current**: Net +0.2%; interior→urban out-migration persistent

## Education
- Estret: 42% post-secondary
- Tramuntana: 12% post-secondary
- Birú: lowest attainment; limited secondary access in highlands

## Health
- Life expectancy gap: Estret 79.2 vs Tramuntana 72.4 (6.8 years)
- Infant mortality: 14/1,000 (higher in interior)
- Birú: higher infectious disease, maternal mortality, malnutrition
