## Etymology (decided by user, 2026-09-16)
- Zong word: 神丹 / ''Sîn-tan'' — "divine cinnabar"
- Chosen from 3 proposed options (divine cinnabar / divine deep / sunken sandalwood); user picked Option 1
- Attributed to Zong mariners of the eastward expansion era — vivid red sunrises over the open ocean likened to cinnabar, the alchemical elixir mineral
- English "Shendan" = corruption of the Zong reading

## Established facts used (sources)
- Largest body of water on Adonia — Adonia article (photo caption)
- Mariana Trench, 10,911 m — deepest location in any ocean — Adonia article
- Oldest oceanic crust (~200 myr) in the Western Shendan — Adonia article
- Shendan Plate moves 52–69 mm/year (fastest-moving tier) — Adonia article
- Bounded by eastern Fosia (Zong seaboard, Jade Peninsula) and western Lurandia — Zong article, First War of the Isthmus
- Sanese Archipelago in northern waters; Yanbaru = maritime chokepoint — Yanbaru article
- Zong eastward expansion across the ocean in 1430s after Jade Sea War exclusion — Yanbaru article, History of Zong (1390–1538)
- Colonies: Xinhai (1456, failed), Anping (1474, modern Louyang), Tamsui (1538, Sinchew) — History of Zong memory
- Great Disappointment (1564) — convergence of Fosian and Illypnian expansion
- Cinc Parets–Macaio galleon trade (est. 1608), landfall at Kerama islands (Yanbaru), terminus Cinc Parets (Dagit) — First War of the Isthmus article
- First War of the Isthmus (1711–1716): Shendan Ocean = primary naval theater — First War of the Isthmus article
