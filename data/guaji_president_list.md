# Presidents of the Second Republic of Guajicutea

Term: 4 years, no term limits. Representatives: 2 years, no term limits.

| # | Name | Party | Term Start | Term End | Notes |
|---|------|-------|-----------|----------|-------|
| 1 | Javier Barirí | PdA (post-RG) | July 1, 1829 | July 1, 1837 | Served 2 terms. Retired July 1, 1837. Died August 1, 1837 (one month after retirement). First president. RG leader during reconstruction (1824–1828). Surname: bari (storm) + -rí. |

## Notes

- First Republic (1723–1812): Cónsuls, not presidents. See `guaji_governor_list.md` for colonial governors.
- Second Republic established October 12, 1828. First election June 12, 1829 (8 months after formation).
- Barirí was RG leader from 1824 (Virtuous Revolution) through 1828 (Second Republic formation), then elected president.
- RG split into PdI and PdA on May 15, 1832. Barirí aligned with PdA (isolationist, post-trauma).
- Economic depression 1834–1838. Lacashe-Asikyira War 1838–1840 shifted sentiment toward PdI.
- PpLT formed 1851 — rural/worker party, began challenging seats.
- PpE (Partido de Estabilización) formed 1861 — protoleftist, protectionist, anti-immigration. Nativist labor party.
- War of Liberation 1853–1856 during Ríos term.
- Guano boom from 1853 — Warikú island (ID 1, "little moon").
- First ironworks in Waribá (1864), Sartan investment.
- Race riot 1866. Immigration ban from Abyala followed.
- Public universal primary education established 1870 under Moku.
- PdI-PpLT secret coalition formed 1872 (PpE had plurality, not majority).
- Surname patterns: mix of Sartan surnames (Paredes, Navarro, Ríos, Aguilar, Cruz, Peña) and Guaji plain-root surnames (Yaya, Karibá, Moku, Tabá). No -í endings.
