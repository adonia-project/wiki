# Kings of Guajicutea — Ruler List

Appendable list of all known rulers of Guajicutea, from unification (516 CE) to present.

Two periods:
1. **Caciques Má** (516–1536 CE) — elected kings, pre-colonial. Symbolic regnal names from Guaji roots.
2. **Boqors** (1536 CE–) — hereditary kings under Burawi vassalage. Baptismal/Christian names.

YOB/YOD columns left blank where unknown. Fill in as information is established.

### End of Boqor line (1609)

The Boqor line ended with the deposing of Carlos I in 1609 and the establishment of the Sarta Colony of Guajicutea. Guajicutea ceased to be a kingdom and became a colonial territory under a Sartan governor.
