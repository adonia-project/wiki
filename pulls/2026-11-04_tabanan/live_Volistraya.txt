'''Volistraya''' is a country occupying the southern portion of the continent of [[Almuria]]. At 2,284,129 square kilometers, it is the larger of the two countries on the continent. Volistraya shares the island continent with [[Pakiak]] to the north, from which it is separated by the <> mountain range.

== History ==
{{Main|History of Volistraya}}

The indigenous peoples of Volistraya share common ancestry with the [[Indigenous Almurians|indigenous Almurians]] of Pakiak, having settled the continent over 50,000 years ago. The southern region developed distinct cultural and linguistic traditions due to its geographic separation from the north.

== Geography ==
Volistraya occupies the southern portion of the Almurian mainland, encompassing a diverse range of landscapes from coastal plains to interior highlands.
