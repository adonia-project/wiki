'''Tabanan''', officially the '''Imperial State of Tabanan''' ([[Tabanese language|Tabanese]]: ''Karahanan Sabyang it Tabanan'') is a sovereign island nation located in [[Almuria]]. It is divided into five federal provinces, two territories, and a capital district. Tabanan has an area of TBD square kilometers with the population of 19 million people. The nation's capital is [[Balete City|Balete]] while the largest city is [[Paralaya]].
