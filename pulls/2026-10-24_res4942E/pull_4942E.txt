'''Adonian Community Resolution 4942E''' is a draft resolution of the [[Adonian Assembly]] submitted by [[Sindewand]] on 24 October 2026. The draft proposes the condemnation of [[Okaiken]] for the authorization of the use of military force outside international law and without a mandate of the [[Adonian Community]], the imposition of economic sanctions on the state, and the imposition of personal sanctions on the Prime Minister of Okaiken, [[Kousuke Ikazaki]]. It was referred to the [[Committee on Resolutions]] on the day of its submission and awaits discussion.

== Background ==

In September 2026, the cabinet of Okaiken authorized the deployment of two destroyers of the SIF Escort Flotilla, including the ISN Shirakumo, and an auxiliary replenishment vessel to [[Alwana]]. The vessels were tasked with escorting eleven Sanese-flagged commercial ferries chartered by the Adonian Community for the evacuation of civilians displaced by the [[Wolffrean War]], under the humanitarian provisions of [[Adonian Community Resolution 4935]]. The deployment operated under the defensive provisions of the Kurosawa Doctrine and remained under sovereign national command; Sanese naval units were barred by cabinet policy from subordination to the coalition headquarters of the Joint Working Group for Peace in Northern Kaftia.

On 16 October 2026, shore artillery of the People's Republic of [[Bonande]] at [[Mabanda]] shelled one of the chartered ferries, the ''Aozora Maru'', wounding three Sanese merchant seamen. On 23 October the House of Commoners of Okaiken convened an emergency debate on the war, and on 24 October it adopted the October Maritime Security and Non-Belligerence Legislative Package. The Emergency Maritime Civilian Protection Act within the package established statutory rules of engagement authorizing the escort warships to intercept incoming ordnance and to return fire against shore batteries actively firing on convoys within a fifteen-nautical-mile radius.

The draft submitted by Sindewand characterizes the deployment and the rules of engagement as the use of military force by a member state acting outside international law and outside the mandate of the Adonian Community. It argues that Resolution 4935 authorized a humanitarian evacuation and did not provide for the deployment of national warships empowered to employ kinetic force, and that the deployment conflicts with the principle, embodied in draft resolutions before the Assembly, that missions of the Adonian Community are to be managed by consensus. The submission followed the vote of 28 September 2026, at which an abstention by Okaiken contributed to the defeat of draft Resolution 4936, which had proposed sanctions on Sindewand over its border closures to White Wolffrean populations.

== Provisions ==

The draft proposes the condemnation of Okaiken for the authorization of the deployment of the escort flotilla and of the rules of engagement enacted by the Emergency Maritime Civilian Protection Act. It proposes general economic sanctions on the state of Okaiken, consisting of trade restrictions and an arms embargo. It further proposes personal sanctions on the Prime Minister of Okaiken, [[Kousuke Ikazaki]], consisting of the freezing of assets and the imposition of travel bans.

== Submission and proceedings ==

Sindewand submitted the draft to the Assembly on 24 October 2026, the closing day of the emergency debate in the House of Commoners of Okaiken. Under the numbering procedure of the Assembly, the draft received the designation 4942E. The preceding designation, 4942D, belongs to a draft submitted by Okaiken with the co-sponsorship of [[Yanbaru]] and [[Dagit]] that proposes deconfliction zones for civilian shipping in the [[Pfein Sea]]. The draft was referred to the Committee on Resolutions on the day of its submission.

As of 24 October 2026, the draft has not entered discussion. Under the legislative procedure of the Assembly, a draft enters discussion if the Committee on Resolutions approves it or if it is co-sponsored by twenty member states. The delegation of Okaiken has described the rules of engagement as defensive, stating that the escort operations are conducted under the humanitarian mandate of Resolution 4935.

On the night of 24 October 2026, the [[House of Commoners of Okaiken]] adopted an [[October 2026 Diet emergency resolution on the Wolffrean War|emergency resolution]] rejecting the draft's characterisation of the escort flotilla, declaring the proposed personal sanctions against the Prime Minister an interference in the democratic processes of a member state, and calling upon member states to withhold co-sponsorship.
