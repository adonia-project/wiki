'''Adonian Community Resolution 4942D''' is a draft resolution of the [[Adonian Assembly]] submitted by [[Okaiken]] on 24 October 2026, co-sponsored at its introduction by [[Yanbaru]] and [[Dagit]], and by [[Echia]], [[Pavia]], [[Tortona]], [[Rufusia]], [[Kavik]] and the [[Vaiahi Islands]] by the end of October. The draft proposes an official system of safe shipping lanes and clear self-defence rules for warships escorting [[Adonian Community]]-chartered refugee ferries in the [[Pfein Sea]], an investigation into the 16 October shelling of the Sanese-flagged ferry ''[[Aozora Maru]]'', and a strict ban on using AC evacuation ships or personnel for a ground invasion of the [[Republic of Wolffrea]].

The draft was introduced following the 16 October shore attack on the ''Aozora Maru'' off [[Mabanda]], the 20 October leak of Okaiken's private shipping-route notifications through [[Breisland]], and reports on 23 October that [[Joint Working Group for Peace in Northern Kaftia]] (JWG-PNK) amphibious landing craft were gathering in the [[Strait of Sindewand]].

== Background ==

=== Attack on the ''Aozora Maru'' and the route-notification leak ===
{{main|Okaiken-Wolffrea relations|JWG-PNK Operations in Northwest Kaftia}}

In September 2026, Okaiken sent two destroyers of the [[Sanese Imperial Forces|SIF]]'s Escort Flotilla, including the ''ISN Shirakumo'', and a supply ship under [[Adonian Community]] operational command to protect eleven Sanese-flagged commercial ships chartered for the Pfein Sea refugee evacuation. After Wolffrea closed its ports to AC ships on 26 September, the Sanese escort ships moved with the evacuation fleet to [[Alwana]].

While Okaiken voted in favour of [[Adonian Community Resolution 4938]] condemning [[Bonande]]'s occupation of [[Mabanda]] and [[Adonian Community Resolution 4941]] suspending Bonande and [[Yaweka]], it voted against [[Adonian Community Resolution 4939]], which had proposed a unified AC ground peacekeeping force, and abstained on [[Adonian Community Resolution 4940]], which authorized the [[Joint Working Group for Peace in Northern Kaftia]] (JWG-PNK) to carry out air and naval strikes outside AC joint command. During the debate on Resolution 4940, Foreign Minister [[Kenichi Mori]] stated that "Okaiken will not vote for a war it does not intend to fight."

On 16 October 2026, during fighting between coalition aircraft and Bonandian coastal guns around Mabanda, artillery fire from the shore hit the Sanese-flagged ferry ''Aozora Maru'', damaging the ship and injuring three crew members. Behind the scenes, Okaiken had been quietly sending the schedule and coordinates of refugee ships to both the JWG-PNK headquarters at [[Joint Base Ajowa]] and, through [[Breisland]], to the Wolffrean government in [[Blumenaue]] so that neither side would fire on civilian ferries by mistake. When that private channel leaked to the press on 20 October, both sides accused the intermediaries of passing targeting intelligence, effectively ending the informal arrangement.

=== Accumulation of coalition forces ===

On 23 October 2026, reports circulated that coalition military forces, including amphibious landing craft, were gathering near the [[Strait of Sindewand]] ahead of a possible ground operation in Bonande or Wolffrea. In [[Okami]], the [[Ikazaki Cabinet]] faced pressure during the [[October 2026 Diet debate on the Wolffrean War|emergency debate in the House of Commoners]] to protect Sanese-flagged ferries and civilian crews after the ''Aozora Maru'' attack while making sure Okaiken's escort warships and chartered ferries in Alwana could not be pulled into a coalition ground invasion.

== Proposed provisions ==

The draft resolution sets out four main measures:
* '''Investigation into the ''Aozora Maru'' attack:''' Directs the [[Adonian Community Observer Mission in Northern Kaftia]] (ACOM-NK) and investigators from the [[Adonian Secretariat]] to examine radar logs and physical evidence from the 16 October shelling of the ''Aozora Maru'', identify which unit fired on the ship, and determine compensation for ship repairs and the injured crew.
* '''Official safe shipping lanes (deconfliction system):''' Replaces private back-channel messages with a public notification system run by ACOM-NK's existing observer staff in [[Alwana]]. Before an evacuation convoy leaves port, ACOM-NK inspects the ships to confirm they carry no weapons or troops, broadcasts their route and transponder IDs to all parties (including to Blumenaue and Bonande through protecting powers and open maritime radio), and declares a 15-nautical-mile no-fire zone around the ships. The system is administered entirely by ACOM-NK's existing civilian and observer personnel and requires no new AC military deployments or mandatory financial assessments.
* '''Self-defence rules for escort warships:''' Gives warships already assigned to the AC-managed refugee sealift clear permission to defend their convoys if a belligerent ignores the 15-nautical-mile safe zone. Specifically, escort warships are authorized to shoot down incoming missiles, drones, or artillery shells aimed at an evacuation ship, and to fire back only to disable the specific coastal gun or boat that attacked the convoy. Escort ships are not permitted to conduct pre-emptive strikes or join offensive raids.
* '''Ban on ground troops and separation from coalition command:''' Keeps all naval escorts and chartered ferries assigned to the refugee sealift strictly under the humanitarian authority of the Adonian Secretariat, separate from the JWG-PNK war command at [[Joint Base Ajowa]]. It forbids requisitioning evacuation ferries or escorts to carry troops or support amphibious landings, and reaffirms that Resolution 4940 does not authorize sending ground forces into [[Wolffrea]] or [[Bonande]].

== Submission and proceedings ==
{{main|Adonian Community legislative procedure}}

Okaiken submitted the draft to the Assembly on 24 October 2026. Under the numbering procedure of the Assembly, because [[Adonian Community Resolution 4941]] was the most recently adopted resolution — and following the failure of drafts 4942A and 4942B to reach quorum and the dismissal of [[Adonian Community Resolution 4942C]] on 21 October — the draft received the designation '''4942D'''.

Because Okaiken is not a sitting member of the [[Committee on Resolutions]] for the stint beginning in October 2026, the draft was referred to the committee for review while Okaiken — joined by early co-sponsors [[Yanbaru]] and [[Dagit]] — circulated the text to gather co-sponsors. During initial consultations with members of the Committee on Resolutions and interested delegations:
* The delegation of [[Jarin]] raised the question of how security guarantees could be enforced given that neither [[Wolffrea]] (suspended in 1968) nor [[Bonande]] (suspended under Resolution 4941) currently sits in the Assembly. The Sanese delegation replied that the draft does not rely on the diplomatic goodwill of Blumenaue or Bonande: broadcasting convoy coordinates openly removes any excuse of "accidental" targeting under international maritime law, while the self-defence rules allow escort warships sailing alongside the ferries to immediately shoot down incoming fire and neutralize any coastal battery that attacks a convoy. Furthermore, Bonande's eventual readmission to the Community under Resolution 4941 remains conditional on respecting safe international passage.
* The delegation of [[Balboa]] inquired whether administering the safe-lane system would require new AC military resources, and requested clearer definitions of the naval self-defence rules. Okaiken clarified that route registration and ship inspections would be handled entirely by the existing observer staff of ACOM-NK in Alwana without new AC military funding or troop assessments, and that the self-defence rules strictly mean shooting down projectiles aimed at refugee ferries and returning fire only against a gun battery or vessel actively shooting at a convoy.
* The delegations of [[Yanbaru]] and [[Dagit]] requested clarification on the practical workflow of the convoy system and its necessity for smaller maritime states participating in the evacuation; both delegations subsequently co-sponsored the measure to ensure that third-country merchant hulls, crews, and nationals aboard the sealift are covered by the 15-nautical-mile protection zone without obligating their governments to join the JWG-PNK military coalition.

=== Growth of the co-sponsorship ===

The [[October 2026 Diet emergency resolution on the Wolffrean War|emergency resolution adopted by the Diet on the night of 24 October]] directed the government to pursue the draft's adoption and to seek co-sponsorships among member states. Within hours, the co-sponsorship grew from three sponsors to nine.

On the evening of 24 October, the delegation of [[Echia]] acceded to the co-sponsorship, followed within hours by those of [[Pavia]], [[Tortona]] and [[Rufusia]]. The four delegations — described in diplomatic reporting as the "Echian block" — circulated a joint memorandum describing the draft as the first text before the Assembly to write the protection of civilian shipping into law rather than leave it to the discretion of belligerents, and citing the exposure of their merchant fleets in wartime waters. The accessions were widely read as a rebuff to draft [[Adonian Community Resolution 4942E|4942E]]; none of the four joined the Sindewand text's co-sponsorship.

Later that night, [[Kavik]] and the [[Vaiahi Islands]] acceded. The Kavik delegation cited the protection of small-state shipping far from home waters and the interests of the Kavik Sanese community; the Vaiahi delegation, representing an East Fosian Sphere partner of Okaiken whose seafarers serve widely aboard Sanese-flagged merchant hulls, described the fifteen-nautical-mile protection zone as "the difference between the sea as a highway and the sea as a lottery."

=== Proposed amendment by Jarin ===

On the evening of 24 October, the delegation of [[Jarin]] formally requested that Okaiken, as the draft's sponsoring state, amend the text to require that the defense of the proposed deconfliction zones be orchestrated by the [[Adonian Community]] itself rather than provided by volunteering member states, in order to streamline coordination of the protection zone. The proposed provision would require vessels engaged in the zone defense to remain AC-flagged and to be volunteered for command under the armed forces command of the Adonian Community. The Jarin delegation's statement to the sponsoring state was direct: "Okaiken must follow international cooperation treaties if all nations are expected to follow the same; they must volunteer their vessels for command under the AC armed forces command."

The request placed the Sanese delegation in a delicate position. The draft as written already places the refugee sealift under the humanitarian authority of the AC Secretariat — the ferries of the lift are AC-flagged, and the escort group at Alwana operates within the AC-managed evacuation operation — but it preserves national command of the escorting warships, in accordance with the defensive provisions of the Ninth Amendment and the separation-of-command provisions of the Emergency Maritime Civilian Protection Act. Diplomatic reporting noted that Jarin's request paralleled the unresolved Burawan objection to the flotilla's sovereign national command, though routed through AC authority rather than the JWG-PNK coalition command; it noted also that the Secretariat already exercised direction over the AC Navy, and had itself directed an additional fleet to the evacuation mission, so that the amendment would extend an existing command arrangement rather than create a new one. The request remained under consideration at the close of the day.

=== Objection of the Zong ambassador ===

The problem was raised independently on the same evening by the Zong ambassador to Okami, in a démarche delivered to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. The ambassador's argument went to the character of the forces rather than their number: Okaiken, the démarche held, cannot act on its own in regard to AC missions that are already being managed by the AC. "The violation of international law is not the adding of additional forces," the démarche stated. "It is in calling the forces self-directed — and the Secretariat themselves have already directed an additional fleet of the AC Navy forces." Diplomatic reporting in Okami read the démarche as a second expression, within a single evening, of the same demand made by Jarin: that the escort group be brought under the direction of the command the AC already exercises over its own naval forces.

With nine sponsors, the draft remained eleven short of the twenty member states required to enter discussion without the approval of the [[Committee on Resolutions]]. By the end of the day, draft 4942E had attracted no co-sponsor beyond its sponsor state, while the co-sponsorship of 4942D had tripled.

Should the draft be confirmed for floor debate and proceed to a final vote of the Assembly, it will receive the official designation '''Adonian Community Resolution 4942'''.
